# **TTRPG Table Dynamics: Simulated Player Profiles**

This document details a simulated, cooperative group of four players. These profiles feature a realistic blend of complementary play styles, overlapping genre preferences, and shared safety boundaries to assist in your campaign preparation and encounter balancing.

## **Profile 1: Elias**

**Experience Level:** Moderate (approx. 4 years)

Elias is a highly narrative-focused player who thrives on character immersion and emotional stakes. He is highly cooperative, always looking for reasons his character would trust and support the party, making him excellent glue for early-campaign roleplay.

* **Play Style:** **Actor / Storyteller**  
  * Prefers acting in-character and engaging deeply with NPC relationships.  
  * Enjoys making suboptimal mechanical choices if they make sense for his character's narrative arc.  
  * Actively seeks out inter-party roleplay during travel or downtime.  
* **Preferred Genres:** High Fantasy, Gothic Horror, Cozy Mystery.  
* **Lines (Hard Boundaries):**  
  * Sexual violence of any kind.  
  * Harm or abuse involving children.  
* **Veils (Soft Boundaries):**  
  * Graphic torture (can happen in the narrative, but must fade to black or be handled off-screen).  
  * Detailed descriptions of gore.

## **Profile 2: Sarah**

**Experience Level:** Veteran (10+ years across multiple systems)

Sarah is deeply familiar with game mechanics and loves solving puzzles, both on the battlefield and in the narrative. While she is an optimizer, she uses her deep system knowledge to uplift the team rather than overshadow them, often helping newer players find cool synergies.

* **Play Style:** **Tactician / Problem-Solver**  
  * Loves complex grid combat, resource management, and utilizing terrain.  
  * Enjoys political intrigue and untangling complex factions.  
  * Appreciates challenging encounters where the party must strategize to survive.  
* **Preferred Genres:** High Fantasy, Cyberpunk, Hard Sci-Fi.  
* **Lines (Hard Boundaries):**  
  * Sexual violence (shares this Line with Elias).  
  * Real-world bigotry (homophobia, transphobia, racism) directed at PCs or used as casual world-building flavor.  
* **Veils (Soft Boundaries):**  
  * Graphic torture (shares this Veil with Elias).  
  * Visceral descriptions of eye trauma/phobia.

## **Profile 3: Marcus**

**Experience Level:** Beginner-Intermediate (approx. 1.5 years)

Marcus brings high energy to the table. He is curious to a fault and loves interacting directly with the GM's world. If there is a mysterious red button, Marcus will press it—not to derail the game, but to keep the momentum going and see what the GM has planned.

* **Play Style:** **Explorer / Instigator**  
  * Highly motivated by uncovering lore, finding secrets, and exploring the map.  
  * Tends to take decisive action when the party is struck by "analysis paralysis."  
  * Enjoys high-stakes scenarios and doesn't mind if his character dies, provided it’s an epic or meaningful death.  
* **Preferred Genres:** Cyberpunk, Sci-Fi Horror, Weird West.  
* **Lines (Hard Boundaries):**  
  * Animal cruelty or the on-screen death of pets/familiars.  
  * Themes of forced institutionalization.  
* **Veils (Soft Boundaries):**  
  * Romance and sexual encounters (prefers a strict fade-to-black for any intimacy).  
  * Extreme, prolonged claustrophobia or being buried alive.

## **Profile 4: Chloe**

**Experience Level:** Beginner (6 months)

Chloe is relatively new to the hobby and is thrilled just to be part of the group. She is naturally cooperative and attentive, preferring to take a supportive role while she learns the ropes. She loves reacting to the crazy things the other players do.

* **Play Style:** **Audience / Support**  
  * Enjoys playing mechanical support roles (healers, buffers) to keep the party alive.  
  * Loves to listen and watch the story unfold; happy to take a backseat in intense roleplay moments.  
  * Responds very well to direct prompts from the GM or other players (e.g., "What does your character think about this?").  
* **Preferred Genres:** Cozy Mystery, Space Opera, High Fantasy.  
* **Lines (Hard Boundaries):**  
  * Animal cruelty (shares this Line with Marcus).  
  * Sexual violence (shares this Line with Elias and Sarah).  
* **Veils (Soft Boundaries):**  
  * Romance involving player characters (shares this Veil with Marcus).  
  * Themes of gaslighting or severe psychological manipulation.

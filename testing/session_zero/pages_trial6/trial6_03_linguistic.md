### **Vesper Cant**

**Phonetic Patterns:**
* **Consonant Tone:** Percussive and sharp, relying heavily on hard stops, glottal releases, and metallic clicks that cut cleanly through radio static and suit comms.
* **Vowel Dominance:** Clipped, central vowels with minimal gliding, keeping syllables discrete and immediately recognizable over noisy channels.
* **Complexity:** Functional two- to three-syllable constructions built from concrete root words, frequently joined into compound terms for mechanical clarity.

**Naming Conventions:**
* **NPC Names:** Krell-Vak, Torg-Rin, Brak-Sera, Dor-Kag, Vex-Thorne. *Rules:* Names join a sharp monosyllabic personal root to a trade, station, or lineage tag using a abrupt consonant bridge or dash.
* **Location Names:** Rust-Grate Nine, Cold-Spur Bank, Iron-Grip Hull, Drift-Lock Four, Black-Vent Void. *Rules:* Structural or navigational terms combined with physical condition markers or numeric sector designations.
* **Faction Names:** The Scrap-Cutters, Rig-Haul Guild, Vent-Keepers, Spark-Weld Line, Cold-Run Crew. *Rules:* Direct action-noun pairings that identify the operational duty or shared hazard of the group.

**Common Idioms & Taboos:**
* **Sayings:**
  * *"Keep the seal tight."* (Stay focused, keep quiet about valuable finds, or look out for your crewmates.)
  * *"Cold hull, warm cup."* (A reminder to appreciate basic comforts while enduring baseline danger.)
  * *"Don't measure the rust."* (Stop complaining about inevitable decay and get to the job.)
* **Linguistic Taboos:**
  * Stating an unverified pressure reading out loud before the sensor cycles twice (considered a jinx on the airlock).
  * Using high-system honorifics or decorative preamble during active salvage ops (viewed as dangerous chatter that clutters emergency comms).

**Influence on Other DNA:**
* Emergency directives and technical work orders are ultra-concise, stripped of pronouns and filler. In communal galley settings, the cadence softens slightly into rhythmic slang, though speech remains punchy and direct regardless of the speaker's mood.

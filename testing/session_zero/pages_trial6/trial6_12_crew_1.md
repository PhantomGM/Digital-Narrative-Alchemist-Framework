### **Profile**

**Appearance & Presence**

Brak-Tally is a broad-shouldered man whose grey-stubbled jaw seems permanently set against a draft. He wears the heavy canvas coat of the Gate-Tally Guild, darkened at the cuffs by hydraulic oil and seal-grease. A brass wrist-comp is strapped over his sleeve, its mechanical stylus clicking rhythmically beneath his thumb like a metallic pulse. 

He smells of recycling-grade ozone, stale tea, and the sharp, chemical tang of cold sealant paste. When he speaks, his voice is a flat, unhurried rasp that effortlessly carries over the roar of station atmospheric vents.

**Personality & Internal Conflict**

Brak-Tally is rigidly structured, diplomatic, and fiercely controlled. He approaches life at Drift-Lock Four as a series of balance sheets: if fuel goes out, scrap must come in, and the station survives another rotation. He treats protocols not as red tape, but as the thin layer of insulation keeping everyone from freezing in the dark. 

Yet, beneath his unflappable stoicism lies a deep, quietly kept contradiction. Off duty, he shows genuine, unvarnished kindness—sharing rare tea bricks with overworked riggers or quietly patching a novice salvager's thermal suit. But the moment he steps behind his berth desk, his mercy vanishes. If an airlock seal fails or a cargo payload is condemned under his stamp, he accepts zero personal responsibility for the outcome. He clings to the rules as an absolute shield: the paperwork was correct, the procedure was followed, and therefore the void killed the crew, not him.

* **Signature Quirk:** He double-taps the casing of his wrist-comp before signing off on any salvage manifest, muttering *"Tally held, drift clear"* under his breath.
* **Vulnerability:** He cannot abide an un-documented anomaly that points to his own error. If shown a mistake in a docket bearing his seal that cannot be blamed on system wear or atmospheric decay, his composure unravels.

*Application in Play:* When faced with a critical atmospheric leak or an unprecedented breach of dock authority, Brak-Tally does not panic or shout. Instead, he locks up completely. His stylus stops mid-stroke, his gaze fixes on his manifest slate, and he begins reciting sub-clauses of the Vesper Station Docking Accord in an unyielding monotone until someone else takes the controls or the immediate crisis resets to a manageable protocol.

**Backstory**

Brak-Tally spent twenty cycles as a freight-purser on the Vault-Strip Line before taking his static posting at Drift-Lock Four. His turning point came aboard the freighter *Iron-Vent Three* during a sudden hull fracture in Grave-Span Nine. Following standard safety protocol to the letter, Brak locked down the aft bulkheads—saving eighty percent of the cargo and the propulsion rig, but sealing three crewmen inside an unpressurized section.

The subsequent Guild inquiry cleared him entirely; his dockets were flawless, his timestamps exact. The experience etched a permanent lesson into his mind: human judgment is fragile and messy, but the docket is blameless. He transferred to station duty, taking over Berth Three, determined to run the safest, most scrupulously documented bay in the corridor. He has maintained that record ever since, offering warmth and hot tea to anyone who needs it, right up until the moment the airlock door needs to be stamped shut.

### **Gamemaster’s Toolkit**

**Strengths & Weaknesses**

* **+** Matchless understanding of station logistics, cargo values, and Vesper Reach trade routes.
* **+** Unshakeable composure in routine diplomatic or commercial disputes.
* **–** Incapable of improvising outside written protocol during structural emergencies.
* **–** Complete refusal to accept personal blame when standard procedures yield tragedy.

**Secrets**

* He keeps an off-ledger storage unit in the station's lower struts containing personal keepsakes salvaged from the *Iron-Vent Three*.
* He quietly alters dock weight allowances for salvage crews who bring back usable filter stock for The Salt-Vent Galley, disguising the discount as standard tare-weight margin.

**Significant Relationships**

* **Vex-Thorne:** A lead engineer for the Choke-Weld Line who regularly tries to bypass dock safety checks, leading to loud, formal standoffs at Berth Three.
* **Mother Hurn:** Owner of The Salt-Vent Galley, with whom Brak-Tally trades spare ration tea for quiet conversation off the clock.

**Notable Possessions**

* *Brass Gate-Tally Stylus:* Heavy, oil-stained, and hand-engraved with tiny notches representing every haul he has cleared without an audit failure.
* *Berth Three Master Slate:* An armored data-pad containing thirty cycles of station docking logs and fuel tariffs.

**Roleplaying Cues**

* **Communication Style:** Formal, precise, and measured. Uses logistical phrasing ("tare weight," "seal integrity," "clearance threshold").
* **Core Vulnerability:** Confronting him with an explicit, mathematical error in his own sealed record. He cannot process personal fault within the system he trusts to protect him, triggering his stillness.
* **System-Agnostic Mechanical Note:** If players submit official Guild documentation before requesting supplies or dock space, Brak-Tally reduces their processing time and fee by 15%. If they attempt to bribe or rush him without forms, he enforces a mandatory four-hour quarantine inspection.

### **Example Interaction**

The atmospheric exchange valve hissed, venting a plume of warm moisture into the cold air of Berth Three. Brak-Tally did not look up from his slate as the crew stepped off the gangway.

*"Pressure verified at point-nine-two,"* Brak-Tally said, his stylus clicking twice against the brass housing of his wrist-comp. *"Welcome back to Drift-Lock Four. You've brought back twelve tons of structural plate and a dead drive core. State your fuel reserve for the log."*

The pilot stepped forward, wiping soot from her suit visor. *"We're running on fumes, Docket-Master. Main tank leaked two units past the Bypass. We need an immediate pump connection before the line freezes."*

Brak-Tally tapped the stylus again, his pale eyes finally rising to meet hers. *"Leaked reserve falls under Form Seven-B. Pump coupling requires a signed clearance stamp and a three-percent hazard deposit. Keep the seal tight, pilot. The form is on the terminal."*

*"We don't have four hours for paperwork!"* she snapped, slamming a gauntleted hand onto his desk. *"The pump will freeze in twenty minutes!"*

Brak-Tally's hands went entirely motionless. His eyes stayed locked on the slate, his expression smoothing into an unreadable, stony mask. *"Sub-section four,"* he repeated softly, his tone entirely flat. *"Hazardous fuel discharge requires Form Seven-B. Sub-section four. Hazardous fuel discharge requires Form Seven-B..."*

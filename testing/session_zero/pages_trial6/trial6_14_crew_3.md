**Role:** Station Air-Lock & Environmental Rig-Tender  
**Alignment:** Neutral Evil

### **Profile**

**Appearance & Presence**

Tor-Vane is a wiry, hollow-cheeked man whose skin bears the gray, salt-cured tint of someone who has spent thirty years breathing reclaimed air. He wears a heavy, grease-darkened canvas work-rig patched with hardened cold-weld resin at the shoulders. His movements are unhurried and deliberately economical, saving oxygen out of decades of habit. A sharp smell follows him everywhere: sharp ozone, mineral grease, and the bitter tang of stewed chicory root.

**Personality & Internal Conflict**

Tor-Vane treats every conversation like an unwanted tariff. He is outspokenly cynical, demanding upfront payment or trade scrap before so much as checking an airlock seal for a docking crew. He holds no love for station guilds, line executives, or starry-eyed salvagers, viewing all allegiances as suicidal foolishness. Yet beneath his self-serving surface lies a frantic, meticulous devotion to his machinery. He refuses to let a seal fail or an environmental scrubber drop below tolerances, working late into the night cycle in total isolation to mend leaks he swore he wouldn't fix without extra coin.

* **Signature Quirk:** He taps every mechanical pressure housing three times with his heavy wrench before turning a key, listening for micro-fractures in the ring of the metal.
* **Vulnerability:** He cannot leave a mechanical hazard unpatched if human lungs depend on it. If shown an active air leak or a failing regulator, his professed selfishness evaporates, forcing him to fix it even when doing so bankrupts his small savings or ruins his standing with station authorities.

**HOW THE TENSION SHOWS (Stress Responses & Social Tells)**

When social or ethical friction arises, Tor-Vane executes a smooth, conversational pivot—shrugging off moral arguments with a dry raspy remark and steering the topic directly toward material costs, scrap weights, or technical realities. However, under direct structural pressure or physical intimidation from corporate officers, his casual detachment breaks down into subtle fidgeting; he avoids eye contact, shifts his weight nervously between his boots, and quietly steps back toward the nearest emergency hatch, looking for an escape route.

**Backstory**

Decades ago, Tor-Vane was a senior seal-engineer for the Vault-Strip Line aboard a bulk freight haul. During a catastrophic hull collision in Grave-Span Nine, corporate officers ordered him to weld a damaged compartment shut, trapping fourteen salvage workers inside to preserve a cargo of heavy machinery. Tor-Vane refused the command, turned his torch on the lock-bars, and broke the seals to pull the crew out. The resulting pressure loss ruined the cargo and earned him a death warrant from the line. 

He fled into the unmapped corridors, shedding his uniform, his contract, and any belief in institutional loyalty. He learned that flags and contracts exist only to decide who dies in the dark. Arriving at Drift-Lock Four as a nameless fugitive, he took up the station’s neglected lock-tender duties. He swore never to bleed for a crew's cause again, yet the horror of that locked compartment left him utterly incapable of permitting a preventable environmental death on his watch.

### **Gamemaster’s Toolkit**

**Strengths & Weaknesses**

* **+** Incomparable mastery of pneumatic seals, lock mechanics, and environmental scrubbers; completely calm during atmospheric decompression emergencies.
* **–** Openly mercenary and suspicious of newcomers; refuses long-term pacts or group loyalties; highly defensive when questioned about his past.

**Secrets**

* He keeps a hidden stash of high-grade Vault-Strip override keys beneath the floorboards of Lock-Bay Three.
* He knows that Drift-Lock Four’s primary atmospheric frame is suffering from structural metal-fatigue that the station overseers have been actively hiding.

**Significant Relationships**

* **Brak-Tally:** Tor-Vane deeply mistrusts the ledger-keeper’s corporate record-keeping, but respects that the man’s numbers are predictable.
* **Vex-Seam:** Frequently clashes with the cutter over scrap priorities, accusing her of risking hull integrity for fast credits.

**Notable Possessions**

* *The Tappet Wrench:* A custom, double-ended brass wrench notched with custom valve measurements, worn smooth by thirty years of handling.
* *Anonymized Sensor Gauge:* A battered, handheld pressure reader stripped of all serial numbers, tuned to detect toxic gas leaks down to parts-per-million.

**Roleplaying Cues**

* **Communication Style:** Speaks in a dry, raspy voice; cuts off flowery speech with blunt engineering realities and frequent references to oxygen costs.
* **Core Vulnerability:** Professional duty toward life-support integrity. Threatening to break a valve or expose an environmental hazard forces him to comply, even against his self-interest.
* **System-Agnostic Mechanical Note:** Tor-Vane grants advantage or a substantial bonus to any checks made to resolve atmospheric leaks or repair airlock systems in his presence, but imposes disadvantage on group diplomacy checks with local station factions due to his caustic attitude.

### **Example Interaction**

*"You want Bay Five pressurized by second bell? That’s four fuel canisters up front, friend. Don't look at me like that—air isn't free out here in the Span, and neither is my time. Oh, you think that seal looks fine? Listen."* 

He taps his brass wrench against the hatch ring three times. A faint, low whistle echoes through the iron. 

*"That’s micro-fracturing in the gasket seat. You take your rig out with that seal, and your lungs turn to cold soup five minutes out from the station. So pay the four canisters, or go breathe vacuum. Your choice."*

### **Adventure Hooks**

* **The Silent Override:** A stranded salvage tender survives a catastrophic docking disaster because someone secretly routed reserve station oxygen to their airlock without logging the transfer. The party discovers Tor-Vane's signature work on the bypass, giving them leverage over the stubborn engineer.
* **The Vault-Strip Warrant:** A corporate bounty investigator arrives at Drift-Lock Four hunting a former line engineer responsible for ruined cargo. Tor-Vane needs an off-station crew to hide his override keys before his workshop is searched.
* **The Cold-Scrubber Run:** Drift-Lock Four's central environmental scrubber suffers a critical core burnout. Tor-Vane knows where to scavenge a replacement inside the hazardous hulk of *Iron-Vent Eight*, but he needs a crew reckless enough to haul it out with him.

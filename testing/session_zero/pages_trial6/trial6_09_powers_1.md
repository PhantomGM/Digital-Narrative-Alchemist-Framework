### **The Vault-Strip Line**

**Role:** Deep-Hulk Salvage Cartel  
**Alignment:** Pragmatic Industrial / Lawful Neutral  

| **Narrative Essence** | **Archetype** |
| :--- | :--- |
| "A burn-torch applied to history before the ice hardens." | The Industrial Prospectors |

---

**Overview:**  
The Vault-Strip Line is the largest organized salvage consortium operating in Grave-Span Nine. While independent tenders scavenge outer hulls for copper piping and loose structural scrap, the Line fields heavy rig-barges aimed directly at the armored vaults, sealed engine blocks, and flight-recorder vaults deep within the corridor's dead hulks. To independent crews, they are overbearing bullies who claim exclusive rights to the richest derelicts; to station authorities at Drift-Lock Four, they are an indispensable supplier of refined alloys and primary machinery parts.

**Ideology & Methodology:**  
* **First Truths:** "Unclaimed mass is dead weight." The Line views every derelict floating through the Reach as a decaying vault whose contents belong solely to whoever possesses the heavy cutting equipment required to open it.
* **Methodology:** They operate by locking heavy industrial tenders directly onto airlocks, welding perimeter stakes across access corridors, and stationing armed rig-cutters to ward off scavengers. Salvaged components are cataloged, towed back to Drift-Lock Four, and released slowly into the market to keep scrap prices high and stable.

**Internal Structure:**  
* **Leadership:** Overseen by Chief Assayer Vex-Kag, a former dockyard structural inspector who calculates haul values down to the credit. Field operations are divided among individual Fore-Cutters—crew bosses who command their own salvage tenders and retain a percentage of each recovered vault.
* **Internal Conflict:** A sharp dispute divides the "Tender-Guilders," who advocate for cautious, methodical hull stripping, and the "Deep-Breakers," a faction of younger riggers who want to rupture volatile reactor housings on military wrecks to reach high-value contraband, regardless of the explosive risks to the surrounding corridor.

**Resources & Influence:**  
* **Control:** The Line owns five heavy plasma cutting tenders, specialized hull-towing rigs, and several long-term warehouse slips on Drift-Lock Four.
* **Reach:** Their leverage is felt at the repair bays. A crew that falls out with the Vault-Strip Line often finds itself unable to buy replacement hull plates or reactor seals at reasonable rates anywhere along the Cold-Drift Bypass.

**Secrets & Shadows:**  
The vast reserve vault held by the Line in Freight-Slip 12—purported to contain pristine fuel cells and emergency generators that guarantee their massive line of credit with station merchants—is mostly filled with rusted, hollowed-out casings weighted down with scrap ballast. A disaster three seasons ago destroyed two primary tenders and destroyed four-fifths of their trade inventory. They have been quietly repainting and reshuffling the same small set of working generators to maintain the appearance of immense capital.

**Internal Contradictions:**  
The Line aggressively enforces salvage claims using legal charters issued by long-dead star systems that no longer exist, insisting that historical maritime precedent grants them exclusive title over derelicts. Yet they refuse to recognize any current station authority or tribunal when independent crews attempt to bring claims against them. They rely entirely on the authority of extinct laws while operating entirely outside living ones.

**Trajectory:**  
* **Pressure:** A primary trade factor on Drift-Lock Four has demanded an in-person physical audit of Freight-Slip 12 before renewing the Line's primary docking permits.
* **If they win:** They secure total control over the salvage rights across Grave-Span Nine, forcing all independent crews to pay heavy license fees or work directly as low-tier subcontractors.
* **If they break:** The false inventory is exposed, collapsing their credit overnight; individual Fore-Cutters will break off with their rig-barges, turning the corridor into a lawless brawl over individual wrecks.
* **The tell:** Line rig-barges begin offering steep discounts on routine structural scrap while abruptly refusing all requests to rent or sell heavy power equipment.

**Adventure Hooks:**  
* **[Hook 1]:** Fore-Cutter Brak-Thorne hires the party's tender to haul a delicate, unexploded reactor casing out of Iron-Vent Eight before a rival Line crew can weld claims to the airlock.
* **[Hook 2]:** An independent crew asks the party to help negotiate or bypass a Vault-Strip Line blockade that has sealed off a high-value freight hulk in Grave-Span Nine.

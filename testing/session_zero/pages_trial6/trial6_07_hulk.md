### **Regional Significance & Reputation**
In Grave-Span Nine, salvagers speak of Iron-Vent Eight over warm cups of chicory with a blend of awe and suspicion. Outer-system crews consider it the premier salvage prize of the corridor, rumoured to hold intact reactor schematics and unrefined core alloy. Local haulers, however, pass along a word of warning: any crew staying aboard past a single shift starts hearing their own names repeated back through the unpowered comm-tubes.

### **Access & Visibility**
Finding the hull requires tracking an erratic gravity pulse near the edge of the sector. The ship drifts without navigation beacons, obscured by a shroud of engine exhaust and loose hull plating. Matching velocity is tricky work; automated thruster bursts fire at irregular intervals, nudging the massive vessel along an unpredictable drift path.

### **Physical Description**
Viewed from an approach craft, Iron-Vent Eight resembles a massive, broken iron whale suspended in shadow. At the primary airlock, the threshold greets visitors with a sharp, metallic taste in the air and a low, rhythmic thrumming felt straight through the soles of magnetic boots. Pliable thermal seals made of coarse, resin-soaked fabric hang loose where previous crews tried to patch blown pressure hatches with whatever scrap was at hand. 

### **History, Origin & Legacy**
Built recently during a rush to expand industrial transit, the ship vanished on its maiden voyage along with its entire crew and manifest. Local legend holds that a sudden energy surge off the bypass swallowed the vessel whole. What remains is a glaring mismatch between intended purpose and reality: designed as an unglamorous bulk cargo hauler, the vessel now functions as a floating repository of strange acoustic echoes and lingering thermal footprints.

### **The Interior: Dangers & Denizens**
No living soul remains aboard, yet the ship is far from quiet. The automated archival deck regularly replays displaced audio signatures—phantom voices calling out routine maintenance checks—that fool motion sensors and trigger security airlocks. Deep near the main drive, an unexploded auxiliary power cell draws erratic currents, throwing spark-arcs across wet deck plates. At Drift-Lock Four, tension brews among the Scrap-Cutters, where a splinter group secretly treats the ship's erratic radio broadcasts as holy signs.

### **Evolution & Trajectory**
The structural strain on the vessel's spine is accelerating rapidly. Plates that held firm last month are buckling under internal torque, and stress fractures are spreading along the lower cargo deck. A crew returning next season is likely to find two drifting half-hulls rather than a single ship.

### **The Central Secret**
Deep inside the primary hold, encased within a reinforced containment cradle, sits a fully operational automated core unit. It is not dead, nor is it corrupted—it simply believes the ship is still in transit to its original destination, quietly firing maneuvering thrusters whenever its internal clock hits an orbital mark.

### **Loot & Lore (Adventure Hooks)**
* **The Manifest Search:** A quartermaster at Drift-Lock Four offers a generous bounty of fuel tokens for the recovery of the original drive schematics locked in the forward vault.
* **The Echo Signal:** A salvage crew recovers an audio file from the comms deck containing a detailed warning about an upcoming grav-wave shift in Grave-Span Nine.
* **The Antenna Feud:** A station mechanic hires the crew to disable the ship's long-range transmitter to keep a rival salvage guild from tracking its location.

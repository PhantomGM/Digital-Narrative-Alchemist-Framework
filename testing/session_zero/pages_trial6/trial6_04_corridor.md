### **Grave-Span Nine**

### Overview
Grave-Span Nine—known across the sector simply as the Dead Corridor—is a silent expanse of floating freighters, shattered orbital platforms, and abandoned tenders trailing across the outer rim of the Vesper Reach. It is a place where space is heavy with forgotten iron and cold vacuum. For salvage crews working out of nearby stations, this corridor represents both their primary livelihood and their steepest operational cost. Crossing it burns precious reaction propellant and wears down environmental seals, but its unplundered hulls hold the raw materials, spare components, and fuel reserves that keep the regional economy alive.

### Geography & Environment
The corridor is defined by its dense, shifting belt of spatial debris and dead hulks. Natural light is pale and distant here, leaving hulls to freeze rapidly once primary power fails. Without thermal management, unshielded vessels cool to dangerous baselines in a matter of hours. Shifting streams of micro-meteoroids and loose metallic scrap drift along gravitational currents generated by nearby gas giants, creating unpredictable navigational hazards. Radar sweeps are routinely obscured by sensor shadows cast by massive superstructures. Plotting a course through the span requires constant RCS thruster adjustments, making fuel consumption a critical factor for any vessel attempting a haul.

### Society & Culture
No permanent cities exist within the drift itself. Instead, the population consists entirely of transient salvage crews, deep-space haulers, and independent riggers living out of their tenders. Culture here is built on shared survival and absolute practical discipline. On comms, long-winded formalities are dropped in favor of clean, brisk reports; chatter during an airlock cycle is considered bad luck. The old phrase "keep the seal tight" serves as greeting, farewell, and reminder to look out for one another. Crews meeting at nearby waypoints often share hot galley stews, trade sensor telemetry on drifting wreckage, and live by the local philosophy of "cold hull, warm cup"—finding warmth and companionship wherever the rig can dock.

### Economy & Resources
Salvage is the sole economic driver of Grave-Span Nine. Crews brave the corridor to harvest high-tensile hull plating, pre-collapse circuit boards, intact air scrubber packs, and unrefined fuel slurry trapped in the deep tanks of ancient freighters. However, the margin between profit and loss is razor-thin. Every burn of thruster fuel, every expended torch tip, and every liter of recycled oxygen eats into a trip's return. High-value finds must be weighed against the logistical cost of securing and towing them back to station docks. 

### Power & Governance
Formal authority stops at the edge of the drift. The Rig-Haul Guild maintains a loose administrative presence at fringe waypoints, registering claim stakes on major derelicts and taxing scrap brought into port. Out among the dead ships, law is a matter of mutual necessity and cold arbitration. Disputes over salvage rights are rarely settled with force—burning precious fuel or damaging a usable hull benefits no one. Instead, ship captains negotiate over short-range radio or submit logs to station marshals upon their return to port.

### Notable Landmarks
* **Iron-Grip Spire:** A colossal, half-shattered orbital shipyard anchoring a major debris cluster. Tenders frequently use its rigid structural girders as a secure mooring post during long operations.
* **Drift-Lock Four:** A massive, dead supertanker wedged tightly between two asteroid fragments. Its upper decks are open to vacuum, but its reinforced lower cargo holds remain sealed and pressurized.
* **Black-Vent Cluster:** A dense field of scorched naval hulls left behind by an ancient orbital engagement. The area is rich in high-grade structural alloys, though residual power surges and volatile unexploded ordnance make working here treacherous.
* **Cold-Spur Waypoint:** A retrofitted deep-space relay platform equipped with external docking clamps and emergency air-refill tanks, maintained by volunteer salvage crews.

### Threats & Challenges
The primary enemy in Grave-Span Nine is resource exhaustion. Running low on reaction mass or life-support reserves leaves a crew stranded in the dark. Decaying hulks present immediate hazards: compromised bulkheads, trapped pressure pockets, and unstable power cores that flare when tapped. Competition between crews can create friction, but the open void enforces a universal law—if a vessel transmits a distress ping, nearby tenders drop their torches and respond, knowing they may need the same mercy tomorrow.

### Regional Secrets
Deep within the Black-Vent Cluster floats an unflagged research vessel whose outer hull construction matches no known civilian or military registry in the Vesper Reach. Its internal compartments remain dark and unbreached, yet navigation beacons across the corridor experience slight timing drifts whenever solar alignment brings the vessel into line with the regional relay array.

### Adventure Hooks
* **The Ghost Signal:** Drift-Lock Four has begun transmitting a automated distress signal using an emergency frequency retired twenty years ago. The beacon’s power signature is remarkably steady, suggesting a functional power source active deep within the pressurized holds.
* **The Sealed Tanker:** A veteran salvage master offers a set of precise drift coordinates leading to an unplundered fuel freighter inside the Black-Vent Cluster, asking only for passage and a hand recovering a sealed personal logbook from the captain's quarters.
* **The Claim Dispute:** Two independent tenders have attached tow-cables to the same intact supply freighter near Iron-Grip Spire. With both crews short on fuel and refusing to cut their lines, an impartial crew is needed to board the vessel, inspect the bridge log, and verify who logged the primary claim stake first.

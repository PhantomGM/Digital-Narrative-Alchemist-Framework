### **The Freight of Last Accord**

### **The Hook**
The steam off the chicory cup in the Salt-Vent Galley is the only thing keeping the chill of Drift-Lock Four out of your fingers when Brak-Tally slides into the booth. 

"The *Iron-Vent Eight* is drifting wide near the edge of Grave-Span Nine," he says, tapping a stained ledger on the deckplate. "She’s a dead haul, mass drifting every six cycles, but her central registry vault is still sealed tight under cold lock. Vault-Strip Line thinks it's just scrap scrapings, but there's a sealed transit casing in hold three. Fetch that casing and bring it back here before the Gate-Tally Guild tags the hull as hazardous drift. Clean haul, simple handoff, warm meal on my tab when you pull back into dock. Keep the seal tight."

### **Background & Context**
Brak-Tally claims the transit casing holds nothing more than forgotten fuel requisitions and expired salvage leases. In truth, the casing contains the original, unredacted registry charter for Drift-Lock Four—documenting shared usage rights that predated the current tariff squeezes enforced by the Choke-Weld Line. 

If brought to light, the charter prevents the Choke-Weld Line from locking smaller crews out of the primary refueling docks. Brak-Tally wants the document to protect his station position, but he avoids telling the party because he fears they will leverage it or panic over the political fallout. Meanwhile, an advance scout team from the Choke-Weld Line has already boarded the hulk, intending to weld the registry vault shut permanently.

### **Core Objectives**
* **Stated Objective:** Board *Iron-Vent Eight*, retrieve the locked transit casing from Hold Three, and return it to Brak-Tally at Drift-Lock Four.
* **Hidden Objective:** Discover the contents of the registry charter inside Hold Three and decide whether to honor Brak-Tally’s quiet claim, negotiate a broader coalition with the Choke-Weld team already aboard, or present the findings publicly to the Station Assembly.

### **Obstacles & Challenges**
* **Automated Defensive Grid:** The hulk's legacy point-defense system still draws trickles of power from auxiliary cells. Engaging these heavy turrets directly with weapons will tear open nearby bulkhead seals or waste precious ammo without breaching their armored plates. The system must be bypassed by working through environmental control ducts or re-routing power feeds at the secondary junction.
* **Choke-Weld Boarding Team:** A armed three-person team led by a stubborn cutter named Vex-Kag is currently working near Hold Three. Entering a fire-fight with them is disastrous—the acoustic shock in the pressurized corridor will trigger hull fractures, and direct conflict turns a commercial dispute into an active feud. The situation requires fast-talking, sharing credit, or using knowledge of the hulk’s unstable mass to negotiate a split salvage permit.

### **Adventure Structure & Flow**
* **First Beat (Boarding & Equalization):** The crew docks their tender against the unstable airlock of *Iron-Vent Eight*. The hull groans under variable mass shifts, requiring careful umbilical calibration before forcing the hatch.
* **Second Beat (The Crawl):** Deep within the derelict, the crew navigates darkened service conduits, dodging automated sentry arcs while monitoring fuel and air usage. Clues aboard reveal that the vault holds political charters rather than spare parts.
* **Third Beat (The Vault Standoff):** Reaching Hold Three, the crew finds Vex-Kag’s team preparing high-grade thermite torches to slag the vault locks. The party must intervene through diplomatic leverage, clever distraction, or technical trade-offs before the records are destroyed.
* **Fourth Beat (The Return & Payout):** Returning to Drift-Lock Four with the intact casing, the crew faces the ultimate choice of how to deliver the charter, setting their reputation among the station factions.

### **Rewards & Spoils**
* **Tangible Payoffs:** A high-grade set of pneumatic salvage cutters, three fully charged auxiliary power cells, and a generous transfer of station fuel credits.
* **Station Standing & Influence:** Rather than mere wealth, successfully resolving the charter dispute earns the crew open credit at the Salt-Vent Galley, priority docking rights at Drift-Lock Four, and the lasting respect of the local Spire-Tenders.

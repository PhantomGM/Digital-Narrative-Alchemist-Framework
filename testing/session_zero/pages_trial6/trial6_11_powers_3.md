### **The Gate-Tally Guild**

**Role:** Station Trade Syndicate & Freight Arbitrators  
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral  

| **Narrative Essence** | **Archetype** |
| :--- | :--- |
| "A scale that weighs every grain, entirely blind to where the grain was harvested." | The Market Custodians |

---

**Overview:**  
The Gate-Tally Guild controls the berths, fuel-transfers, and trade manifests on Drift-Lock Four. While rival factions fight over what lies buried in the derelict hulks of Grave-Span Nine or argue for sealing the dead corridor entirely, the Guild cares about neither. They care about who pays berth taxes, who buys station coolant, and who keeps cargo moving through the airlocks. To the common salvage tender, they are the bureaucratic necessity that keeps the lights on and the tea hot in the galley; to rival corporate powers, they are the stubborner-than-iron gatekeepers who refuse to give up station sovereignty for any price.

**Ideology & Methodology:**  
The Guild’s foundational belief is simple: *The weight is the truth.* If a vessel’s ledger matches its draft, if its docking fees are paid in valid tender or refined salvage, and if its crew refrains from firing thrusters inside the berth ring, the Guild guarantees passage and trade rights. They operate with absolute indifference to the origin or morality of cargo. A crate of pre-collapse medical filters pulled from a shattered hulk carries the exact same docking fee as a crate of fresh hydroponic tubers from a deep-system transport. They enforce this neutrality through administrative lockouts, control of the station's main fueling booms, and a modest fleet of heavily armored harbor-tugs.

**Internal Structure:**  
Power is vested in the Board of Tally-Masters, chaired by Tally-Director Vex-Kag—a woman who has managed station weight-ledgers for thirty cycles and knows the weight of every hull in the Reach by sight. The Guild is divided into the Dock-Keepers (who manage berths and airlocks) and the Ledger-Clerks (who audit cargo manifests and manage the station fuel reserves). 

An internal rift is widening between the "Fluidists," who want to lower tariff barriers to attract informal, unregistered independent runners, and the "Tariff-Purists," who insist on raising fees to build up a station defense fund against corporate encroachment.

**Resources & Influence:**  
The Guild controls all primary docking berths, high-volume fuel manifolds, and weigh-stations across Drift-Lock Four. They hold the long-term supply contracts for station-wide life support consumables, meaning every shop, tender, and galley—including The Salt-Vent Galley—relies on Guild-certified meters to keep air and water flowing. Their influence is felt at every airlock seal, where their brass solder-stamps mark inspected freight.

**Secrets & Shadows:**  
The Guild’s core reserve of high-grade hull sealant and reactor coolant is nearly exhausted. The exorbitant docking fees and strict fuel rationing imposed on incoming vessels are not driven by greed or expansionism, but by a frantic effort to purchase emergency chemical shipments before station-wide life support systems suffer a catastrophic pressure drop. Every administrative delay and fee hike is cover for an account balance that is running on fumes.

**Internal Contradictions:**  
The Gate-Tally Guild preaches absolute open-market neutrality and free-trade access for every spacer in the Reach, yet they maintain a ruthless, unyielding monopoly over berth assignments and fuel valves. This friction is a relic of the station’s founding charter: to keep the corridor open to all, the original dock-workers had to seize complete control of the station's physical choke points. Today, small salvage crews frequently find themselves priced out or locked out by the very organisation that claims to guarantee their right to trade.

**Trajectory:**  
* **Pressure:** Rising import costs from deep-system fuel suppliers, combined with the Choke-Weld Line’s periodic threats to seal off incoming shipping lanes.
* **If they win:** Drift-Lock Four becomes an independent, self-sustaining free-port city, but high entry tariffs turn small independent tenders into permanent indentured berth-tenants.
* **If they break:** The Guild loses control of the docks; berths are seized by private salvage crews and corporate guards, turning the station’s central spine into a series of barricaded, toll-charging fiefdoms.
* **The tell:** Harbor-tugs running on reduced thruster power, and hand-lettered paper surcharge notices pasted directly over the heavy brass tariff seals on berth airlocks.

**Adventure Hooks:**  
* **The Ledger Audit:** Tally-Director Vex-Kag hires the crew to haul a discreet shipment of emergency sealant from a distant waypoint, bypassing official customs ledgers to prevent station panic.
* **Berth Seizure:** The Guild impounds a crew’s primary tender over an unexpected, retroactive "corridor maintenance tax," forcing the players to negotiate a salvage barter, earn a favor with the Ledger-Clerks, or sneak aboard to override the dock-locks.

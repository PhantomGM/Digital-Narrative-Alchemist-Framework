### **The Choke-Weld Line**

**Role:** Corridor Quarantine & Hazard Containment Union  
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral  

| **Narrative Essence** | **Archetype** |
| :--- | :--- |
| "A heavy iron rivet hammered into a leaking hull." | The Reluctant Blockaders |

---

**Overview:**
The Choke-Weld Line is a specialized fraternity of deep-space riggers, safety heralds, and structural engineers who maintain that Grave-Span Nine is an operational catastrophe waiting to cascade. Their ultimate goal is to permanently cap both ends of the dead corridor with explosive-severed bulkhead anchors, shutting down transit through the span entirely. To local salvage crews, they are stubborn killjoys spoiling profitable runs; to habitat safety boards in neighboring sectors, they are the thin line keeping unstable derelicts from drifting into populated shipping lanes.

**Ideology & Methodology:**
* **First Truths:** *"An open valve bleeds the ship."* *"Containment before profit."* The Line holds that uncontained wrecks act as decay anchors, dragging down surrounding trade routes and risking catastrophic reactor ruptures if left unmonitored.
* **Methodology:** They deploy heavy structural clamps, long-range warning buoys, and thermite-welding tenders to constrict movement through the corridor. While they offer bounties for crews who turn in verified hazard logs, they physically weld shutdown collars over the airlocks of derelicts they deem beyond safe operating limits.

**Internal Structure:**
* **Leadership:** Led by Weld-Captain Vex-Dorn, a veteran engineer who survives on plain protein paste and mint tea after surviving a fatal bulkhead collapse early in his career. Authority is distributed among regional "Canton Chiefs" stationed at major transit bottlenecks.
* **Internal Conflict:** A bitter rift divides the **Cap-Firsters**—who want to detonate the corridor's entry bulkheads immediately, even if it seals active salvage crews inside—and the **Phased-Welders**, who advocate for a scheduled, multi-cycle withdrawal that gives legitimate crews time to clear out.

**Resources & Influence:**
* **Control:** They command heavy industrial tenders fitted with hull-bracing gantry arms, high-output thermal welders, and long-range warning transponders.
* **Imprint:** Their presence is announced by high-visibility hazard tags stamped across derelict hatches, thermal-welded steel bars blocking unstable corridors, and automated beacons pinging cautionary signals across Vesper Cant frequencies.

**Secrets & Shadows:**
The Choke-Weld Line knows that the aggressive salvage operations run by their rivals, the Vault-Strip Line, are accidentally keeping several derelict reactors from going critical by venting core pressure during strip-downs. If the Choke-Weld Line succeeds in completely capping the corridor without first bleeding those reactors, three massive hulks in the central span will build up thermal pressure and detonate, destroying the primary jump-anchor of the neighboring sector. Leadership keeps this quiet while desperately trying to design a covert venting routine before dropping the final seals.

**Internal Contradictions:**
The Line's doctrine demands closing Grave-Span Nine to eliminate structural hazards, yet they fund their fleet, pay their riggers, and purchase equipment by charging "containment fees" and selling hazard-clearance passes to the very salvage vessels operating inside the corridor. If they achieve their founding goal and seal the corridor forever, their income stream instantly vanishes, leaving them unable to pay their crews or maintain their ships.

**Trajectory:**
* **Pressure:** A sudden structural shift in the unstable freight-hulk *Iron-Vent Eight* has nudged its drift path directly toward the Cold-Drift Bypass, threatening to pin multiple tenders against the station unless stabilized or capped.
* **If they win:** The entry gates are dropped and sealed with heavy weld-seams. Denied their primary revenue source, the Line collapses financially within weeks, leaving hundreds of armed, unpaid riggers stranded around Drift-Lock Four.
* **If they break:** Their blockade collapses under financial pressure, and the Line fractures into competing hazard-salvage gangs who sell forged clearance stamps to any crew with cash.
* **The tell:** Heavy hazard-stenciled iron plates welded over every secondary docking latch on Drift-Lock Four, accompanied by the constant hum of warning transponders echoing through the station halls.

**Adventure Hooks:**
* **The Brace Job:** Weld-Captain Vex-Dorn hires the crew to haul a set of heavy structural braces into the fractured interior of *Iron-Vent Eight*, keeping the hulk stable long enough for his engineers to complete a hazard survey.
* **The Sealed Vault:** The Line has welded a heavy containment collar over the outer airlock of a derelict containing high-value salvage the party was contracted to retrieve. The crew must convince the local Canton Chief to grant a temporary breach permit or find a way around the barrier without setting off thermal trip-beacons.

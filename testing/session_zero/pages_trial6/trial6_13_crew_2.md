### **Profile**

**Appearance & Presence**

Vex-Seam is a lean, stooped woman whose shoulders bear the permanent curvature of forty years spent crawling through half-meter utility ducts. Her skin is weathered like oiled leather, pitted with tiny star-shaped scars from cold-weld sparks, and perpetually smudged with black graphite grease. She wears a heavy canvas work apron bristling with brass calipers, pressure gauges, and roll-pins over a faded, heavily patched pressure undersuit. 

She moves with deliberate, economical quiet, accompanied by the dry metallic clatter of tethered tools against her hip and the persistent, sharp tang of ozone, solder flux, and lukewarm chicory tea that trails in her wake.

**Personality & Internal Conflict**

Vex is fiercely kind, scrupulously honest, and deeply devoted to keeping the people of Drift-Lock Four breathing. If an apprentice collapses from exhaustion or a transient salvage rigger sustains a seam-burn, she will sit up for two straight shifts nursing them, brewing herbal tea, and offering gruff, steady comfort without expecting a word of thanks. 

Yet the moment the conversation turns to physical supplies, a cold, obsessive friction takes hold. She is pathologically miserly with station hardware. She will patch a dying seal for free, but she will fiercely demand three credits for the copper gasket used in the repair, counting out change with ruthless precision. She treats every bolt, roll of sealant, and spare valve as if it were a drop of her own blood.

* **Signature Quirk:** Before returning any hand tool or brass fitting to her belt, she compulsively taps it three times against her palm while quietly reciting its component serial number.
* **Vulnerability:** She cannot abide unmetered or unrecorded scrap. Accusing her of hoarding for personal gain or demanding she "donate" spare fittings for the station’s greater good triggers an immediate, defensive panic.

**HOW THE TENSION SHOWS (Stress Responses & Social Tells)**

* **High Strain (The Leak):** When pressed under extreme tension or confronted with a sudden supply deficit, Vex’s calm composure cracks. Her hands develop a pronounced, rapid tremor, her raspy voice rises an octave, and she launches into aggressive, breathless over-justifications of her price sheets while furiously recalibrating her hand-held pressure gauge.

**Backstory**

Thirty years ago, Vex served as a junior pipe-fitter aboard the haul tender *Veritas-Seven*. During a routine transit through a dead corridor, an auxiliary pressure manifold blew its main seal. The engineering deck had plenty of replacement collars, but the quartermaster had quietly given away three boxes of unlogged gaskets to an unclassed refugee barge two stations back. When the blowout happened, there were no spare seals left in the bin. Vex held a manual valve shut with her bare hands for six hours while three of her closest crewmates suffocated ten feet away behind a locked emergency bulkhead.

She survived, but the event shattered her understanding of generosity. To Vex, an uncounted part or a freebie isn't a kindness—it is a murder weapon waiting to go off. She came to Drift-Lock Four, set up her workshop in the damp utility crawlspaces, and resolved that no line under her care would ever fail, and no scrap of brass would ever leave her inventory unbilled.

### **Gamemaster’s Toolkit**

**Strengths & Weaknesses**

* **+** Masterful knowledge of atmospheric plumbing, structural hull stress, and pneumatic systems.
* **+** Absolute moral integrity; she will never lie about structural safety or cheat on technical diagnostics.
* **–** Completely incapable of offering volume discounts or waiving material costs, even to friends in dire straits.
* **–** Highly pessimistic about hardware longevity, often insisting on immediate, costly preventative maintenance.

**Secrets**

* She has secretly stashed four military-grade air-scrubber cores inside a sealed, unmapped conduit behind the galley—bought entirely out of her own meager docked wages over fifteen years.
* She holds a quiet, unfulfilled debt of gratitude toward the Choke-Weld Line for towing her off the dead *Veritas-Seven*, though she regularly berates their field inspectors over component overcharging.

**Significant Relationships**

* **Brak-Tally:** A frequent sparring partner. She respects his ironclad station record-keeping, but grows furious whenever he suggests writing off lost salvage inventory as general depreciation.
* **Marn-Kettle:** The head cook at The Salt-Vent Galley. Vex repairs Marn’s tea boilers for zero labor cost, but relentlessly hounds her for the exact cost of replacement pipe fittings.

**Notable Possessions**

* *The Seam-Striker:* A custom-weighted pneumatic torque-wrench with a worn bone handle, fine-tuned to detect internal metal fatigue by sound alone when tapped against a bulkhead.
* *The Tally-Spool:* A brass-bound, oil-stained ledger recording every nut, bolt, washer, and seal brought into or used on Drift-Lock Four over two decades.

**Roleplaying Cues**

* **Communication Style:** Raspy, pragmatic, and fast-talking; frequently uses plumbing and pressure metaphors ("Don't blow your burst-disc over a slow drip").
* **Core Vulnerability:** Her stinginess regarding physical supplies. Pressure applied to her material reserves or suggesting she is "price-gouging" hits her core trauma. She will over-justify her receipts violently rather than admit her terror of running out of parts during a breach.
* **System-Agnostic Mechanical Note:** When repairing player equipment or ship hulls, Vex guarantees maximum reliability, but her material costs are always 20% above standard market rate. She refuses to haggle on parts; lowering her price requires an empathetic appeal to her safety concerns rather than a commercial negotiation.

### **Example Interaction**

Vex doesn't look up from her bench as you approach. The rhythmic *clack-tap* of her calipers against a copper elbow pipe continues unabated. 

*"Hold your breath for a second—no, seriously, quiet. Hear that faint whistling behind the bulkhead panel? That's two hundred liters of scrubbed air a day bleeding into the ductwork because whoever installed this flange used a cheap lead washer instead of braided brass."*

She sets the pipe down, wipes her greasy fingers on her apron, and holds out a small, gleaming ring of metal. 

*"I can seat the proper brass ring in five minutes. Labor's free—I won't watch good folk breathe stale tank-rot. But the ring costs three credits. Not two. Not two and a half. Three credits, placed right here on the bench before I open my tool-roll."*

When you suggest she split the cost for station safety, her hands freeze. Her fingers begin to twitch against her belt, her voice tightening as she leans in. 

*"Split it? You think I'm pocketing this? You think copper grows on the structural ribs? If I don't bill for this ring, I can't buy the next ring, and when the main manifold cracks, we all sit in the dark turning blue! Three credits. Right now. Or buy a bucket and catch the air yourself."*

### **Adventure Hooks**

* **The Ledger of Missing Seals:** A station-wide audit reveals three high-capacity atmospheric valves are missing from Vex’s official workshop registry. Gate-Tally Guild inspectors accuse her of selling military scrap to privateers, unaware she has hidden them in an unmapped duct as an emergency backup for the station.
* **The Cold-Joint Bargain:** A independent scrapper brings in a section of breached plating from *Iron-Vent Eight* containing rare pre-collapse alloy seals. Vex desperately wants them to reinforce the station's weakest airlock, but the scrapper demands a trade she cannot afford without player intervention.
* **Crawlspace Leak:** A silent, dangerous pressure drop begins flickering on the station's secondary line in Grave-Span Nine. Vex needs a crew willing to squirm into a hazardous, unpressurized utility duct to guide her pneumatic welder while she patches the seam from the inside.

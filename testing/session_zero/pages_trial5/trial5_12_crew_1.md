**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral

### **Profile**

**Appearance & Presence**

Kaelen is a broad-shouldered figure wrapped in a heavy, grease-stained deck coat that still bears the faded, rectangular outlines where Ribbon-Way Consortium patches were violently torn away. His face is a map of easy, windburned smiles and a silver-capped canine that catches the harsh fluorescent hum of Tether-End’s docking bays. He moves with a slow, deliberate stride, his magnetic boots clanking rhythmically against the deck plating. 

The sharp, medicinal tang of industrial solvent and cheap clove tobacco follows him like a personal atmosphere. In his right hand, he almost constantly rolls a heavy brass mechanical tally-counter, its metallic *click-click-click* acting as a constant rhythmic backdrop to his presence.

**Personality & Internal Conflict**

Kaelen is a walking paradox: a boisterous, back-slapping extrovert who is entirely, chillingly stoic beneath the noise. He is the first to buy a round of chicory-ale at the Butter-Burner and the loudest voice in any toast, yet his amber eyes remain flat, observant, and entirely unreadable. 

His defining contradiction lies in his profound, practical kindness paired with an utter lack of empathy. Kaelen will hand a desperate, half-starved salvage crew a crate of high-grade atmospheric filters without asking for a single credit—an act of genuine, unforced generosity. Yet, if that same crew begins to weep or thank him, his face goes blank. He does not care about their suffering; he simply decided they should have the filters. 

Furthermore, while he possesses ironclad personal integrity and never tells a lie, he is profoundly irresponsible. He treats his official duties as cargo master with creative negligence, letting critical station maintenance logs pile up while he pursues his own schemes.

*   **Signature Quirk:** He clicks his brass tally-counter exactly three times before answering any direct question about cargo, debts, or his past.
*   **Vulnerability:** He is deeply insecure about his fall from corporate grace. Any reminder of his failure within the Ribbon-Way Consortium’s formal logistics division will cause his boisterous facade to instantly evaporate.

**HOW THE TENSION SHOWS (Stress Responses & Social Tells)**

Kaelen’s commitment to order is absolute, but his moral compass is highly flexible. Under high strain—such as when his conflicting loyalties are exposed or a salvage deal goes disastrously wrong—he experiences **The Leak**:

*   His booming voice drops to a rapid, defensive whisper. His hands begin to tremble, forcing him to pocket his brass tally-counter to hide the shaking. He will aggressively over-justify his actions, reciting page, line, and verse of Tether-End’s maritime cargo codes or the exact wording of a salvage contract to prove he did nothing legally wrong, even if his choices resulted in a crew’s ruin.

**Backstory**

Kaelen was once a rising star in the Ribbon-Way Consortium’s logistics corps, managing supply chains across the Dead Corridor. He believed in the company, the schedules, and the math. That belief died when a corporate restructuring abandoned an entire sub-sector of miners—including his younger brother—to save on fuel costs. Kaelen didn't rebel; he simply checked out. He took a demotion to Tether-End, resolved to never care about a grand cause again.

His current position is defined by **quiet complicity**. He has watched Tether-End decay for a decade. Rather than fight the inevitable, Kaelen has quietly signed a private agreement with the Sovereign Reclamation Trust. He is actively, honestly, and legally helping them buy up the station’s outstanding debts, ensuring a slow corporate foreclosure. He doesn't hate the station; he has simply decided it is a sinking ship, and he is making sure he—and the select crews he chooses to favor—have dry seats in the lifeboats.

### **Gamemaster’s Toolkit**

**Strengths & Weaknesses**

*   **+ Master of Logistics:** He knows the location, price, and history of every bolt and fuel rod in the Dead Corridor.
*   **+ Unflinching Honesty:** He does not lie. If he promises a crate of rations, it will be there, exactly as described.
*   **– Existential Apathy:** He has zero interest in saving Tether-End or fighting corporate greed; he believes resistance is a mathematical waste of time.
*   **– Professional Negligence:** His paperwork is a chaotic disaster, often resulting in lost shipments and administrative gridlock.

**Secrets**

*   He is the anonymous broker who leaked the security bypass codes of the active relic-hulk, *The Clay-Shorn Pilgrim*, to the Sovereign Reclamation Trust.
*   He keeps a hidden, physical ledger of all the station officials currently on the Trust’s payroll inside an old, hollowed-out Consortium battery pack in his office.

**Significant Relationships**

*   **Quartermaster Marra (NPC):** His nominal superior. She is desperately trying to keep Tether-End independent, and Kaelen constantly undermines her efforts through deliberate administrative incompetence.
*   **The Sovereign Reclamation Trust (Faction):** His secret employers, who view him as a highly useful, if somewhat erratic, inside asset.
*   **The Butter-Burner’s Cook (NPC):** His primary drinking buddy and the only person on the station who knows Kaelen is actively helping the Trust.

**Notable Possessions**

*   **The Brass Tally:** An antique mechanical counter. He uses it to track "favors" he is owed by various crews.
*   **Consortium Signet Ring:** A heavy silver ring containing a high-grade security override key, a relic from his corporate days.

**Roleplaying Cues**

*   **Communication Style:** Loud, blunt, and highly informal. He uses spacer slang and corporate jargon interchangeably. He never beats around the bush.
*   **Core Vulnerability:** If confronted with the human cost of his complicity with the Trust—especially if it mirrors the abandonment of his brother—his stoic armor will crack.
*   **System-Agnostic Mechanical Note:** Kaelen cannot be caught in a lie because he does not tell them. However, if players use his paperwork chaos against him, any check to search his cargo manifests or bypass station customs is made with a significant advantage.

### **Example Interaction**

Kaelen slaps a heavy, condensation-slick pitcher of chicory-ale onto the rusted metal table of the Butter-Burner, pulling up a stool with a screech of iron.

"Drink up, boys. The local vacuum doesn't care if your throats are dry, but I do," he bellows, his silver tooth flashing. He slides a clean, sealed crate of medical supplies across the floor with his boot. "There's your penicillin. No charge. Consider it a gift from the Cargo Office's rounding errors."

The pilot blinks, hesitant. "Kaelen... this is worth three runs of scrap. What's the catch? Who do we owe?"

Kaelen’s smile remains wide, but his eyes are suddenly stone-cold, completely devoid of warmth. He clicks his brass tally-counter three times in his pocket. *Click. Click. Click.*

"You don't owe anyone. I wanted you to have it, so you have it. But if you're asking me to help you smuggle those fuel rods past the Trust's new blockade tomorrow? Absolutely not. I've already signed the clearance logs for their patrol cutters. They'll search you, they'll find them, and they'll impound your ship. It's on page four of the harbor treaty. Don't look at me like that, kid. I didn't write the physics of the corridor; I just sign the manifests."

### **Adventure Hooks**

*   **The Missing Reactor Core:** Tether-End’s life support is failing. Kaelen has "misplaced" the replacement parts in his chaotic warehouse. He offers to pay the party to "salvage" them from a nearby derelict—which is actually a Sovereign Reclamation Trust supply depot he wants cleared out.
*   **The Paper Trail:** Quartermaster Marra suspects there is a mole in the administration. She hires the party to break into Kaelen's chaotic cargo office and find proof of his double-dealings before the next Trust representative arrives.
*   **The Brother’s Ghost:** A survivor from the mining sector collapse where Kaelen’s brother died arrives at Tether-End, carrying information that suggests the Ribbon-Way Consortium’s abandonment was actively facilitated by Kaelen's own logistics reports.

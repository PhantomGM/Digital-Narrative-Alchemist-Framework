### **The Butter-Burner**

**A cramped, grease-warmed galley** in **Tether-End** — kept by **Marrow**

| **Essence** | **Archetype** |
| :--- | :--- |
| "A fragrant, flour-dusted sanctuary where the low hum of the station fades behind the smell of sizzling onions and the quiet murmur of tired crews." | Station Galley and Neutral Haven |

### **Atmosphere**
The moment you crack the hatch of The Butter-Burner, the harsh, metallic tang of Tether-End’s recycled air vanishes. In its place is a thick, intoxicating wave of toasted yeast, browning butter, and hot chicory. Inside, the room is packed shoulder-to-shoulder, yet it is remarkably quiet. Instead of the usual rowdy tavern shouting, patrons speak in low, exhausted murmurs. It is a space of decompression, where salvage crews fresh off the run sit hip-to-hip on benches worn smooth by grease-stained flight suits, finding quiet solidarity in their shared fatigue.

### **Goods & Services**
While Marrow serves hearty, thick stews and fresh-baked bread, the real draw is the comfort of things made by hand. Because the station’s official supply manifests are strictly regulated, Marrow quietly uses unregistered, salvaged nutrient-pastes and black-market spices to give his dishes their rich, homestyle flavor. A thick bowl of grey-lentil porridge with a generous heel of crusty sourdough costs exactly three copper chits. For those returning from a particularly brutal run, Marrow will quietly slip a spoonful of real berry jam onto their plate—no questions asked, and no extra charge.

### **Personnel & Influence**
The galley is run by Marrow, a retired salvage engineer with a squeaky pneumatic prosthetic hand, who wants nothing more than to source a steady supply of real, un-recycled sea salt to perfect his sourdough crust. He is helped by a rotating crew of off-duty deckhands who chop onions and scrub stockpots in exchange for a warm meal and a quiet corner to sleep. Because of its reputation as a peaceful sanctuary, even the high-ranking navigators and regional salvage bosses respect the threshold. Weaponry is left in the rack by the door, and corporate disputes are checked at the hatch.

### **Secrets & Threats**
The Butter-Burner holds no dark conspiracies under its deckplates, though it does harbor a few harmless secrets. Tucked away in the flour pantry is Barnaby, a beach-ball-sized, six-legged dust-guzzler. Rescued by a salvage crew from a corporate research vessel’s scrap pile, Barnaby is a gentle, silent creature that vacuums up spilled flour and purrs like a faulty transformer when scratched behind his sensory stalks. 

The galley's only real hazard is its ancient, over-engineered fire-suppression system. Repurposed from an old cargo bay, the system is incredibly sensitive. If a pan smokes too much or someone raises their voice in anger, the ceiling vents instantly flood the room with a thick, sticky, lavender-scented fire-retardant foam. It is harmless, but cleaning it out takes hours, making it a legendary station joke.

### **Interdependencies**
The Butter-Burner’s warmth relies heavily on the cargo haulers of the Iron Ribbon to bring in fresh flour and real spices from the inner systems. Additionally, Marrow maintains a quiet, respectful arrangement with the local Order of the Resonant Utterance. A monk of the Order regularly visits to bless the yeast starters—a ritual Marrow swears keeps the dough rising in low gravity—in exchange for a permanent corner table reserved for silent meditation.

### **Evolution Over Time**
Though it is a relatively new addition to Tether-End, built into a salvaged utility corridor, the galley has rapidly become the emotional heart of the station. As more crews realize it is the one place they can drop their guard, its influence is quietly growing. Marrow has recently begun clearing out an adjacent storage locker to set up a few permanent cots for crews who need a safe, quiet place to sleep off the adrenaline of the dead space.

### **Adventure Hooks**
* **The Flour Run:** A trade vessel carrying a rare shipment of real-gravity grain and Marrow's coveted sea salt has broken down two sectors away. Marrow is offering a lifetime of free meals to any crew willing to tow them in before the cargo spoils.
* **The Lavender Sabotage:** A petty corporate middleman, angry at being barred from the galley for harassing a deckhand, plans to sneak in and trigger the over-sensitive fire-suppression system during a vital gathering of salvage captains. Marrow needs someone to watch the vents and catch him in the act.

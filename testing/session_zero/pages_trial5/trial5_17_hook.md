### **The Bleeding Hull of the Sovereign Dawn**

### **The Hook**

The air in the Butter-Burner is thick with the smell of scorched grease and cheap synthetic chicory. A nervous man in a frayed corporate uniform slides into your booth, his eyes darting toward the entryway. He speaks in a ragged, desperate whisper.

"Look, the Sovereign Reclamation Trust doesn't know this transponder is pinging yet. They think the *Sovereign Dawn* is just another overdue scout vessel lost to the drift. But I know what she was carrying. If you get your tender out there within the hour, you can board her, pluck the heart right out of her reactor, and be back before their heavy cruisers even transition into the corridor. I don't want a cut of the salvage—I just want the encrypted flight logs. Deliver them to me, and I'll wipe your crew's docking debt from the station's ledger. Do we have a deal, or do I find another crew with a working airlock?"

### **Background & Context**

The *Sovereign Dawn* was not on a routine scouting mission. It was a black-budget research vessel testing a highly unstable, experimental power source. Three days ago, the ship suffered a catastrophic internal event and drifted into a debris field. 

The corporate clerk who hired you, Master Orlov, claims he is merely trying to save his career by recovering the logs before his superiors realize he leaked the vessel's coordinates. In truth, Orlov is deeply in debt to local syndicate lenders on Tether-End and plans to sell the flight logs to a rival faction. He has deliberately omitted the fact that the ship’s experimental reactor did not merely fail—it ruptured, tearing a localized rift that has attracted the attention of the Shiver-Veneer. The vessel's hull is structurally intact, but the interior has begun to warp, humming with a sickening, resonant frequency that distorts gravity and physical space.

### **Core Objectives**

* **The Stated Goal:** Board the drifting scout ship, extract the primary flight logs from the bridge terminal, and salvage the standard fuel cells.
* **The Hidden Truth:** The crew must stabilize or eject the experimental reactor core before its fluctuating containment field collapses entirely. Additionally, they will discover the ship's cargo bay holds the illegal prototype core itself—a prize of immense value, but one that is highly volatile and actively hunted.

### **Obstacles & Challenges**

The *Sovereign Dawn* is far from a passive graveyard. The ship's automated security grid is fully pressurized and active. Heavily armored defense turrets sweep the main corridors, and internal blast doors have sealed off the direct paths to the bridge. 

Attempting to fight through these automated defenses with standard salvage laser-cutters or personal sidearms is a swift path to a cold grave. The security plating is designed to shrug off small-arms fire, and the turrets will easily overwhelm a direct assault. 

Instead, the environment demands a more calculated approach. The crew must navigate the narrow, unmapped maintenance conduits running behind the bulkheads. By locating and splicing regional power junctions, they can trick the ship's automated sensors, vent specific rooms to disable turret tracking, or reroute the security grid to open blast doors. 

Furthermore, the deeper decks are infected by the Shiver-Veneer. The crystalline growth has fused with the ship's wiring, causing localized gravity fluctuations and spatial distortions. The crew must use their environmental scanners and sound-baffling gear to navigate these warped zones without triggering a resonance cascade that would alert the hostile growths.

### **Adventure Structure & Flow**

* **The Cold Boarding:** The adventure begins with a tense maneuvering sequence as the players match their salvage tender's velocity with the tumbling, dead scout ship. Docking is a delicate dance; the outer airlock manual override is jammed, requiring a clever bypass from the outside while managing finite suit oxygen.
* **The Silent Grid:** Once inside, the crew enters a silent, freezing environment. The air is stale, and the metal beneath their boots vibrates with a low, distressing hum. As they push toward the auxiliary engineering bay to restore localized power, the ship's automated defense grid flickers to life, forcing them to find alternative routes through the service crawlspaces.
* **The Warped Galley:** As they near the mid-decks, the environment shifts. The geometry of the corridors becomes visibly distorted—straight lines curve unnervingly, and loose tools float in localized pockets of zero gravity. The crew must solve a complex power-routing puzzle at a damaged sub-station to isolate the bridge's security lockouts while avoiding the crystalline patches spreading across the deck plates.
* **The Core Purge:** Reaching the reactor bay, the difficulty and complexity spike dramatically. The experimental core is on the verge of a resonance collapse, and the Shiver-Veneer has woven itself directly into the containment housing. The ship's automated emergency protocols initiate a ten-minute countdown to a complete reactor purge. The crew must execute a frantic, multi-step manual override across three separate decks simultaneously, balancing the risk of radiation exposure against the clock to secure the valuable core and escape before the ship's internal systems self-destruct.

### **Rewards & Spoils**

While the immediate, tangible reward promised by Orlov is the wiping of their station debts and a modest pile of standard fuel cells, the true prize lies in the experimental core itself. If successfully salvaged, this high-grade prototype can be sold on the black market for a staggering sum of credits, or installed directly into the players' salvage tender, permanently upgrading their thruster efficiency and reducing their fuel consumption during long runs.

In addition to the physical salvage, the crew can recover a pristine, uncorrupted navigational database from the bridge. This data contains the coordinates of three previously unmapped derelict hulls deeper within the Dead Corridor—valuable leads for future salvage operations. 

Securing these logs also earns them the lasting respect of Bess "The Barnacle" Marrow, who recognizes the signature of the experimental core and offers to act as a discreet broker for their newly acquired corporate secrets.

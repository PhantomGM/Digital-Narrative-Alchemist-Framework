### **The Shiver-Veneer**

**Classification:** Swarm, Nanite-born — **Threat Apex**
**Prevalence:** Rare, localized to deep-corridor derelicts

| **Field Note** | **Archetype** |
| :--- | :--- |

### **Description**

**Body & Movement**
The Shiver-Veneer does not possess a single, discrete body. Instead, it is a massive, undulating colony composed of billions of microscopic, needle-like metallic segments. Together, they behave as a single, fluid colossus that slithers across bulkheads like poured mercury. It moves with a heavy, liquid grace, flowing through cable runs, ventilation shafts, and structural seams. 

It is entirely blind, possessing no optical sensors. Instead, it navigates by detecting minute fluctuations in thermal energy. You will hear it before you see it: its passage creates a heavy, cacophonous hum—a bone-rattling vibration that resonates through a ship's structural metal. As it draws near, the ambient temperature drops sharply, and electronic displays begin to flicker and warp.

**Behaviour & Hunting**
The Shiver-Veneer is entirely mindless. It possesses no malice, no culture, and no desires; it operates purely on mechanical reflex and thermal attraction. It is drawn inexorably toward heat and electrical activity. A running generator, a lit blowtorch, or the warm life-support suit of a salvager will draw its attention. 

Its signature behavior is "plating." When dormant or stalking, the swarm spreads itself paper-thin across the interior of a hull, perfectly mimicking the ship’s own structural ribs and plating. Only a slight, silvery sheen betrays its presence. When a heat source passes nearby, the "wall" liquifies and flows toward the target in a silent, relentless tide.

**Ecology**
This entity acts as an apex decomposer within the cold tombs of the Dead Corridor. It feeds on electrical currents and thermal energy, slowly breaking down refined starship alloys into a fine, inert grey powder. It reproduces by fission; when a colony absorbs enough thermal energy, it buds, splitting into two distinct swarms. 

Because the dead hulks are mostly cold and silent, its population is dwindling. However, the few surviving colonies have become highly concentrated and reactive, adapting to aggressively seek out the brief, intense heat signatures introduced by active salvage crews.

**The Anomaly**
The swarm is animated by a degraded nanite-slurry of forgotten origin. Its sheer density produces a powerful, localized infrasonic field. This vibration triggers a profound sense of dread, mild nausea, and auditory hallucinations in sapient minds—a phenomenon crews call "the shiver." This is not a psychic attack, but a physical side effect of the nanites maintaining cohesion.

### **Gamemaster's Toolkit**

**Threat & Tactics**
The Shiver-Veneer does not strike with claws; it smothers. It flows over warm machinery and suits, drawing out heat and electrical power until the target is frozen and inert. It exploits the narrow corridors of derelicts to cut off escape routes, pouring through floor grates to seal exits with a dense wall of cold, vibrating metal.

**Weakness**
For all its metallic resilience, the nanite-slurry relies on delicate electrostatic bonds. Simple, un-ionized water completely disrupts this charge. Drenching the swarm causes it to lose cohesion instantly, collapsing into a harmless puddle of inert grey sludge and rusted needles.

**Signs of Its Presence**
*   **The Frost:** Bulkheads coated in a fine, silver-grey metallic frost that feels unnaturally cold to the touch.
*   **The Hum:** A low, vibrating resonance in the deckplates that can be felt through boot soles.
*   **Battery Drain:** Suit power and flashlight batteries draining twice as fast as normal.

**Salvage**
If a swarm is successfully neutralized with water, the calcified nanite-skeletons can be swept up and collected. This dense, metallic dust can be refined into highly valuable superconducting filaments, highly prized by the Ribbon-Way Consortium.

**System-Agnostic Mechanical Note**
*When active, the Shiver-Veneer drains 1 unit of Power/Fuel from any active device (flashlights, plasma cutters, life support) in its zone every round. Anyone standing in its zone must succeed on a tension check or succumb to the infrasonic "shiver," suffering a penalty to all actions due to severe vertigo and dread.*

### **Adventure Hooks**

*   **The Cold Shoulder:** The crew boards a derelict transport, *The Gilded Shiver*, only to find its reactor is offline but still warm. The ship's hull is entirely coated in a dormant Shiver-Veneer that is slowly waking up as the crew's suits radiate heat.
*   **A Slippery Cargo:** A representative from the Sovereign Reclamation Trust offers a massive bounty for a "live" sample of the slurry, contained in a specialized thermal-insulated flask. The flask, however, has a slow leak, and the station's galley is starting to feel very cold.

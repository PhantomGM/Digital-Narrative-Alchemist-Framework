### **The Sovereign Reclamation Trust**

**Role:** Monopoly Salvage Syndicate  
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral  

| **Narrative Essence** | **Archetype** |
| :--- | :--- |
| "A golden ledger bound in heavy iron plates." | The Entitled Monopoly |

---

**Overview:**
The Sovereign Reclamation Trust (SRT) is a massive, heavily armed corporate syndicate that claims exclusive legal ownership of every scrap of metal, relic, and historical record within the Dead Corridor. Operating under an ancient, sweeping charter, they view the independent crews of Tether-End not as hard-working survivors, but as "unlicensed scavengers" and "contractual parasites." While they possess the raw firepower to sweep the corridor clean, doing so is logistically ruinous. Instead, they squeeze the region through high-pressure blockades, legal intimidation, and buying out desperate crews, establishing themselves as the shadow that hangs over every salvage run.

**Ideology & Methodology:**
The Trust operates on the "Doctrine of Prior Title." They believe that because the ancient empires that built the derelicts fell, the rights to their remains defaulted to the Trust’s corporate predecessors. They do not merely want scrap metal; they want the high-value relics, ancient ship logs, and navigational data buried deep within the hulks. 

To achieve this, they employ a mix of bureaucratic strangulation and targeted military force. They deploy massive "Reclaimer" corvettes to blockade high-value targets, forcing independent salvagers to either pay exorbitant "licensing fees" or watch their hard-won hauls be seized as "contraband."

**Internal Structure:**
The Trust is organized like a military bureaucracy, overseen by the Board of Appraisers. The local operations in the Dead Corridor are directed by Auditor-General Vaelen Vance. Power is strictly top-down, enforced by the "Securitat"—a private military branch that handles asset protection. 

However, a quiet schism is forming between the **Logisticians**, who believe the most cost-effective path is to peacefully buy out Tether-End and turn the local crews into cheap, contracted labor, and the **Securitat Officers**, who advocate for a violent campaign of asset seizure to clear the corridor of "infestations" once and for all.

**Resources & Influence:**
The Trust controls heavy-class salvage platforms, long-range patrol corvettes, and massive processing barges capable of stripping a derelict to its skeleton in days. Their greatest weapon, however, is their leverage over the Iron Ribbon. By lobbying the core-world suppliers, they can artificially inflate the price of fuel, air, and spare parts arriving at Tether-End, quietly suffocating those who refuse to sign their exclusivity contracts.

**Secrets & Shadows:**
The Trust's terrifying legal and military front is a carefully maintained illusion. In truth, their ancient charter is set to expire in less than a standard year, and their parent company has quietly frozen their operational budget. They are running on fumes. Every aggressive blockade, every legal threat, and every patrol is a desperate bluff. They must find a legendary, game-changing relic—such as the central archive of *The Clay-Shorn Pilgrim*—to prove the corridor's profitability to their distant investors before the funding is cut entirely, leaving their fleet stranded in the dark.

**Internal Contradictions:**
The Trust preaches absolute legal precision, corporate honor, and the sacred nature of contract law. Yet, their local officers routinely falsify salvage boundaries, backdate ownership claims, and declare "emergency quarantine zones" over active wrecks just as independent crews arrive. This hypocrisy is not born of simple malice, but of desperate middle-managers trying to meet impossible corporate quotas under the threat of demotion and abandonment.

**Trajectory:**
* **Pressure:** A looming audit by the parent company's oversight vessel, which is currently making its way down the Iron Ribbon to review the Trust's dwindling profit margins.
* **If they win:** They will establish a total monopoly over the Dead Corridor, turning Tether-End into a bleak company town where independent crews must lease their own air from the Trust.
* **If they break:** The corporate budget is cut completely, causing the Securitat to mutiny and turn their heavy warships into high-tech, desperate pirate bands that prey on the very trade routes they once protected.
* **The tell:** Trust-branded beacon clamps attached to the hulls of newly discovered wrecks, pulsing with a warning signal that declares the vessel "Property of the Sovereign Reclamation Trust."

**Adventure Hooks:**
* **The Exemption Clause:** Auditor-General Vance quietly approaches the party's crew. The Trust's heavy, slow-moving salvage suits cannot navigate the shifting, razor-sharp debris of a newly discovered hulk. He offers the party a "one-time legal exemption license" to retrieve a specific logbook from the wreck—but the Securitat is watching, ready to seize the prize the moment the party exits the airlock.
* **The Quarantine Bluff:** The party arrives at a lucrative wreck only to find a Trust patrol boat claiming the area is under "bio-synthetic quarantine." The party must determine if the threat is real, find a way to spoof the patrol's sensors, or negotiate a cut with a corrupt Appraiser who is willing to look the other way for a bribe of fresh galley food from The Butter-Burner.

### **Profile**

**Appearance & Presence**

Bess is a broad, weathered woman of middle years, her skin permanently stained with grease and carbon-dust. She wears a heavy, multi-pocketed utility coat that clinks with loose brass fittings, copper washers, and small pressure gauges. Her hair is a wild, silvering nest held back by a pair of cracked welding goggles resting on her forehead. 

She carries the scent of ozone, stale chicory, and the sharp, metallic tang of cold solder. Her voice is a low, gravelly rattle, like gravel being turned in a cement mixer, and she rarely looks anyone in the eye for long, preferring to stare at the mechanical parts they brought her to appraise.

**Personality & Internal Conflict**

Bess is a walking paradox: a fiercely honest, deeply empathetic soul who would rather chew glass than admit she cares about the salvage crews. She is relentlessly kind to the broken-down spacers who limp into Tether-End, yet she hoards high-grade atmospheric scrubbers and copper wiring like a starving rodent. She is outspoken when defending the station’s common folk against corporate suits, yet when actual physical violence breaks out, she is the first to duck behind a bulkhead, cursing her own trembling knees.

- **Signature Quirk:** She keeps a jar of mismatched, stripped screws on her counter. Whenever she is forced to negotiate or make a hard deal, she compulsively sorts them by thread-count, only to dump them back together when she is finished.
- **Vulnerability:** She cannot bear the thought of being responsible for another structural or mechanical failure. If a crew brings her a piece of critical, failing station hardware, she will trade away her most prized personal reserves to secure a replacement, even if it ruins her profit margin.

**HOW THE TENSION SHOWS (Stress Responses & Social Tells)**

Bess’s commitment to keeping the station’s independent crews alive while defying corporate law is an exhausting tightrope. When the pressure peaks—such as when the Sovereign Reclamation Trust threatens to cut the station's fuel supply, or when a salvage run goes disastrously wrong—the cracks in her gruff exterior split wide open. 

Under this high strain, Bess experiences **The Leak**. Her hands begin to shake violently, making it impossible for her to solder or sort her screws. She will weep silently while continuing to work, or aggressively over-justify her hoarding of parts with frantic, defensive rants. She will lecture anyone nearby on the exact statistical probability of their collective suffocation, her voice cracking as she tries to convince herself she has everything under control.

**Backstory**

Bess spent her youth as a junior logistics officer for the Sovereign Reclamation Trust, auditing the very scrap-yields she now brokers. Her life was defined by slow ideological corruption, making small compromises for her corporate masters until the night of the "Great Venting" of Sector Four. She saw the catastrophic pressure failure coming in the data but failed to report it, fearing she would lose her pension. When the bulkheads blew, her cowardly instincts took over; she fled to the escape pods instead of pulling the manual override, leaving her young apprentice to choke in the dark.

She fled the Trust's payroll and grafted herself onto Tether-End like a barnacle. Her sharpest contradiction—her absolute honesty and fierce kindness paired with a pathological greed for mechanical parts—was born in that disaster. She knows exactly what a single missing copper seal costs in human lives. She will not lie to a spacer about the value of their haul, but she will swindle the Trust out of every spare bolt she can pry loose, hiding them in her private lockers against the day the air runs thin again.

### **Gamemaster’s Toolkit**

**Strengths & Weaknesses**

* **+** Unmatched eye for salvage value and structural integrity
* **+** Deep, practical knowledge of Tether-End’s jury-rigged life support systems
* **–** Pathological hoarding of critical parts, occasionally depriving others of immediate resources
* **–** Physical cowardice in active combat or physical confrontations

**Secrets**

* She has a hidden crawlspace behind her shop containing three illegal, military-grade atmospheric scrubbers—enough to keep the station's common deck breathing for a month if the main grid dies.
* She still secretly pays a portion of her broker fees to an anonymous account, attempting to support the family of the apprentice she left behind in Sector Four.

**Significant Relationships**

* **Kaelen Vance:** She views the young salvage captain with a mix of maternal dread and professional respect, constantly worrying he'll get himself killed for worthless junk.
* **Auditor Thorne:** Her former Trust supervisor who still occasionally monitors Tether-End's manifests, whom she actively bribes with doctored scrap reports to keep her past hidden.

**Notable Possessions**

* *The "Trust-Buster" Calipers:* A heavily modified set of digital calipers that can bypass Trust-encrypted cargo locks.
* *A dented brass pocket watch:* It no longer keeps time, but contains a charred piece of her apprentice's uniform ribbon.

**Roleplaying Cues**

* **Communication Style:** Speaks in a rapid, gravelly clip, throwing in technical salvage jargon; frequently interrupts herself to mutter about maintenance.
* **Core Vulnerability:** Accusing her of hoarding at the expense of others' immediate safety will instantly trigger "The Leak."
* **System-Agnostic Mechanical Note:** When players trade with Bess, she will offer 20% more for mechanical parts, but will absolutely refuse to sell or trade any atmospheric or life-support components unless the players succeed on a difficult social check or prove a dire station emergency.

### **Adventure Hooks**

* **The Air-Tax Audit:** Auditor Thorne is arriving at Tether-End for an unannounced inspection. Bess needs the party to help her move or disguise her massive, illegal stockpile of life-support spares before the Trust seizes them.
* **The Cold Run:** The station's primary heat-exchanger is failing. Bess knows where a replacement can be scavenged within the *Clay-Shorn Pilgrim*, but she is too terrified of the derelict's tight spaces to go herself and needs the crew to retrieve it.
* **A Promise in the Scrap:** Bess accidentally brokers a piece of salvage containing an encrypted log-disk from Sector Four, detailing the corporate cover-up of the disaster she fled. She needs the party to help her decode it, but doing so will draw the deadly attention of Trust recovery agents.

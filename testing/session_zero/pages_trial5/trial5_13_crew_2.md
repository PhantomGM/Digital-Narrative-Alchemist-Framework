### **Profile**

**Appearance & Presence**

Sariel is tall, sharp-angled, and moves with the quiet efficiency of a piston. She wears a grease-stained but meticulously pressed uniform of the Ribbon-Way Consortium, her collar pinned with a tarnished brass seal of office. Her fingers are permanently stained with purple ledger ink, and a heavy ring of iron docking keys hangs from her belt, clinking softly against a mechanical tally counter. 

Her presence is defined by a dry, chemical scent—a mixture of old paper ledgers, ozone, and the bitter clove oil she uses to keep her mechanical counter from seizing. When she speaks, her voice is a flat, rhythmic rasp, like dry parchment sliding over stone.

**Personality & Internal Conflict**

Sariel is the spine of Tether-End’s supply chain. She is polite, highly diplomatic, and speaks with a structured, formal precision that brooks no interruption. Beneath her calm, stoic exterior lies a frantic, obsessive drive to keep the station's numbers balanced. She is deeply insecure about her own position, masking her fear of failure behind an unyielding devotion to protocol. 

Her greatest internal contradiction lies in her split approach to morality. She is capable of profound, practical kindness—she will quietly allocate extra oxygen scrubbers to a struggling crew's tab or ensure a sick spacer's family gets fresh rations—but she is absolutely merciless if they try to negotiate their debts or appeal to her emotions. She is personally stingy, refusing to spare a single coin from her own pocket, yet she manages the station's communal charity funds with religious devotion and absolute fairness. 

* **Signature Quirk:** She never uses a digital slate for her master inventory, keeping everything in a massive, leather-bound paper ledger. She claims digital files "drift like the hulls."
* **Vulnerability:** If her ledger is defaced, lost, or proven to contain an error she cannot explain away, her rigid facade shatters.

**HOW THE TENSION SHOWS (Stress Responses & Social Tells)**

* **Magnitude:** Apex (9)  
* **State:** Absolute  
* **Physical/Social Tell:** **The Freeze**

When confronted with an undeniable error she committed, or when physically threatened by violence in her docks, Sariel undergoes an unnatural, terrifying stillness. She does not flinch, shout, or run. Instead, her hands lock onto her ledger with white-knuckled force, her eyes glaze into a clinical, unblinking stare, and she begins reciting the *Tether-End Port Authority Salvage and Indemnity Act of 294* in a flat, unyielding drone. She remains completely unresponsive to external stimuli—even a gun pointed at her chest—until the threat is removed or she is physically moved from the space.

**Backstory**

Sariel’s rigid worldview was forged in the fire of quiet complicity. Decades ago, she was a low-level clerk for the Sovereign Reclamation Trust during the disaster known as the Great Decompression of Sector 4. Faced with a rapidly spreading atmospheric leak, she was forced to make a triage decision: she sealed a cargo bay containing three of her closest colleagues to save the station's main atmosphere tanks. 

To protect the Trust and her own skin, she lied in her subsequent report, blaming a faulty automatic valve. The lie saved her career and the station, but the guilt left her permanently scarred. She is absolutely unaccountable for her own errors, refusing to ever admit a mistake, but she has dedicated her life to ensuring the numbers never lie again. She believes that only absolute adherence to the ledger can prevent the void from swallowing Tether-End.

### **Gamemaster’s Toolkit**

**Strengths & Weaknesses**

* **+** Flawless organization, absolute fairness in trade, deep knowledge of derelict cargo manifests.
* **–** Completely inflexible under pressure, paralyzing fear of personal blame, zero tolerance for chaotic improvisations.

**Secrets**

* The master ledger contains a secret "Ghost Column" where she tracks fuel she has systematically skimmed from corporate vessels to keep the station's life support running.
* She knows Kaelen Vance's true history but keeps it quiet because his survival keeps her own supply lines stable.

**Significant Relationships**

* **Kaelen Vance:** A tense, professional alliance. She respects his survival instincts but despises his disregard for paperwork.
* **Warden Corin:** Her direct corporate overseer in the Ribbon-Way Consortium, whom she despises but obeys.

**Notable Possessions**

* *The Iron Ledger:* A massive, metal-shod book containing the true manifest of every scrap of metal on Tether-End.
* *The Brass Tally:* An antique mechanical counter that she uses to count down the minutes of air left in docking bays.

**Roleplaying Cues**

* **Communication Style:** Extremely formal, using maritime and salvage jargon. Refers to players by their vessel registration or cargo class rather than names.
* **Core Vulnerability:** Accusing her of personal corruption or showing her a mathematical error in her books.
* **System-Agnostic Mechanical Note:** If the players try to negotiate fuel or supply costs, they cannot roll for a discount unless they present a physical, written loophole in the station's charter.

### **Example Interaction**

*"Your vessel is registered as the *Gilded Shiver*, yes? You are three liters short on your fuel tax, Captain. I cannot release your docking clamps."*

The captain steps forward, slamming a hand onto the counter. *"We barely made it back! The coolant lines are frozen, my crew is coughing up black dust, and you're talking about three liters of fuel?"*

Sariel does not blink. Her hand remains resting on the open page of her ledger, her fingers perfectly still. 

*"The coolant lines are indeed unfortunate,"* she says, her voice as flat as a calm sea. *"But the air you are currently breathing belongs to the dry-dock reserve. If I release your clamps without payment, the residents of Ring 3 lose twelve minutes of pressure tomorrow morning. I am sure they would thank you for your sacrifice, but they cannot breathe gratitude. Pay the tax, or the clamps remain closed."*

### **Adventure Hooks**

* **The Discrepancy:** Sariel hires the party to retrieve a specific manifest from a high-threat derelict, but warns them that if they bring back even one unlisted crate, she will impound their entire ship.
* **The Ghost Column:** A corporate auditor from the Ribbon-Way Consortium is coming to inspect Tether-End. Sariel needs the party to "create a temporary distraction" in the docking bay so she can balance her secret fuel reserves.
* **The Price of Air:** The station's scrubbers fail. Sariel calmly prepares to vent an entire residential ring to save the core. Can the party find a mechanical solution before her timer hits zero, or will they have to break her freeze?

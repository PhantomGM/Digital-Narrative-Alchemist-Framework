### **The Silence at Shaft Four**

### **The Hook**
The speaker is Garrick Vance, a shift boss whose knuckles are white as he grips a tin mug of cold chicory in a back corner of the Cog & Kettle. He leans in so close you can smell the coal dust and cheap peppermint on his breath.

"Look, I didn’t go to the Magistrate, and I sure as hell didn’t go to the Cartographers. They’d just seal the drift and write off my crew as acceptable losses. But five men—good men, men who know how to handle a pick—went down into Shaft Four six hours ago. The steam hoist is still turning. The air-pumps are chugging. But the signal bell has gone dead-silent. And when I put my ear to the speaking tube... it didn't sound like a cave-in. It didn't sound like water. It sounded like a wind blowing through a pine forest. We're a mile below the grass, friends. There ain't no pine trees down there. I need you to go down, find my boys, and bring 'em up before the night shift arrives and someone else notices the silence."

### **Background & Context**
On the surface, this is a straightforward rescue mission to recover a stranded mining crew. Garrick Vance claims he just wants his crew back, but his panic stems from a deeper fear: he knows they breached a sealed imperial vault-line. 

Beneath the mundane iron deposits of Shaft Four lies a remnant of the old high-fantasy empire—a localized resonance pocket. The miners didn't suffer a physical collapse; instead, they unearthed an ancient acoustic seal. The pocket has begun to bleed a mesmerizing, low-frequency hum into the tunnel. While the Magistrate wants the iron extraction to continue without disruption, and the Chthonic Cartographers would gladly sacrifice the miners to study the anomaly, the truth is that the "song" of the deep is already changing those who listen to it. The miners are not dead, but they are no longer entirely themselves.

### **Core Objectives**
*   **The Stated Objective:** Descend into the depths of Shaft Four, locate the five missing members of the mining crew, and return them safely to the surface.
*   **The Hidden Objective:** Discover the source of the anomalous acoustic hum and stabilize the leaking imperial seal before the vibration propagates upward, threatening the structural integrity of the upper mining levels.

### **Obstacles & Challenges**
*   **The Resonance Mites:** The cavern walls are thick with these crystalline pests. They have been whipped into a frenzy by the imperial seal's vibration. Attempting to fight them with blades or bullets is a fatal mistake; kinetic impacts cause the mites to shatter into explosive, sonic fragments that trigger chain reactions. Instead, the party must bypass them by dampening their own footsteps, using thick grease to coat their gear, or distracting the creatures with localized heat sources.
*   **The Mesmerizing Sough:** The air itself carries a sweet, pine-scented draft and a low, harmonic vibration. Anyone who listens to it for too long feels an overwhelming urge to sit down, close their eyes, and join the chorus. This mental hazard must be resisted through focus, ear-plugs fashioned from candle wax, or by maintaining a loud, rhythmic counter-noise—such as shouting Gritspeak work-songs or banging iron tools in a steady cadence.

### **Adventure Structure & Flow**
*   **The Descent:** The journey begins in the rumbling iron cage of the steam hoist. As the party descends, the familiar clatter of Glimmerfall fades, replaced by an eerie, deadening silence. The temperature drops, and the scent of damp earth is slowly replaced by the impossible smell of fresh pine needle and ozone. The transition is smooth but deeply unnatural, establishing a tense, quiet atmosphere.
*   **The Quiet Gallery:** At the bottom of the shaft, the party finds the miners' tools neatly stacked against the timber supports, as if they simply laid them down and walked away. There are no signs of struggle, but the crystalline formations along the walls are vibrating in sync. Here, the party must navigate their first encounter with the highly reactive Resonance Mites, learning quickly that violence only amplifies the danger.
*   **The Singing Seam:** Deep within the drift, the party discovers the missing miners. They are sitting in a circle around a narrow, glowing fissure in the stone, their eyes wide and glassy, humming a perfect, three-part harmony that matches the vibration of the rock. Scattered around them are high-grade glimmer-ore deposits and discarded imperial surveying tools—a tempting, valuable haul, but retrieving it requires careful coordination so as not to startle the catatonic crew.
*   **The Resonance Break:** As the party attempts to move the miners, the fissure begins to widen, the humming rising to a deafening, bone-rattling pitch that threatens to bring the ceiling down. To escape, the party cannot rely on brute force. They must solve the acoustic puzzle of the imperial seal, using abandoned brass tuning forks to match and cancel out the vibrations, sealing the rift just enough to break the spell and allow a hasty retreat before the gallery collapses.

### **Rewards & Spoils**
*   **The Tangible Payoffs:** A heavy sack of raw, high-grade glimmer-ore salvaged from the seam, and a set of old brass imperial tuning forks that can be used in the future to temporarily quiet sonic anomalies or disrupt crystalline creatures.
*   **The Overall Significance:** While the material wealth gained is modest, the social capital is immense. Saving the miners earns the lasting gratitude of the Hearthkeepers, who look after the working families of Glimmerfall. Furthermore, Garrick Vance promises to keep the party's involvement quiet from the Magistrate, granting them a discreet, trusted ally who can smuggle them down into the deeper, restricted levels of the Descent in the future.

**Prevalence:** Widespread in the Deep

### **Description**

**Body & Movement**
Resonance Mites are minuscule, often no larger than a miner's thumbnail, resembling intricate, beetle-like constructs of dull, metallic grey. Despite their small size, their chitinous casings are surprisingly robust, capable of withstanding significant impact. They move with a slow, deliberate scuttling across surfaces or through the very rock itself, carving shallow burrows with an unknown mechanism. Lacking eyes, they navigate and perceive their environment through a constant, high-frequency chittering and clicking, a cacophony that echoes through the deep tunnels of the Glimmerfall Descent. This continuous, metallic din is the most reliable sign of their presence, often preceding any visual confirmation.

**Behaviour & Hunting**
These creatures are patient ambushers, lying in wait for any source of warmth to pass within range. Their aggression is low; they do not pursue or actively hunt, preferring to remain hidden until a target comes to them. Once a warm-blooded creature is detected, a mite will latch on with surprising tenacity, its tiny form binding itself to skin or clothing. It then begins to siphon away vital energy, a process often described by victims as a sudden, profound chill or a creeping exhaustion. Resonance Mites are highly gregarious, operating as a vast, loosely coordinated collective. While individual mites are small, a concentrated swarm can quickly debilitate even the strongest individual, leaving them weak and disoriented. They are most active during periods of low or changing light, or in the perpetual twilight of the deep mines.

**Ecology**
Resonance Mites infest the deeper, less-traveled sections of The Glimmerfall Descent and the wider Sunken Scar. They are not solitary entities; rather, they exist as components of a pervasive, roaming collective that shifts and spreads through the subterranean environment. Their "diet" consists of vital energy, which they graze from living organisms, often without immediately killing their hosts. They reproduce through live birth, though the process is slow, yielding only a few offspring at a time. This slow individual propagation, combined with their widespread prevalence, suggests their numbers are less a result of rapid breeding and more a testament to their ancient origins and persistent nature as a remnant of a bygone era.

**The Anomaly**
The uncanny nature of the Resonance Mites stems from their nanite-born origin, relics of a forgotten high-fantasy empire. They are believed to be a degraded or repurposed form of pre-Collapse technology, their current function as energy parasites an aberrant evolution from their original purpose. Their ability to drain vital energy is not magical in the traditional sense, but an intrinsic function of their peculiar construction. The exact mechanisms of their creation and the full scope of their original design remain a profound mystery, often debated by the Chthonic Cartographers and the Custodians of the Breach.

### **Gamemaster's Toolkit**

**Threat & Tactics**
Resonance Mites pose a cumulative, insidious threat rather than an immediate lethal one. They attack in swarms, each mite contributing to a growing sense of fatigue and enervation in their target. They exploit the unwary, the isolated, and those already weakened by the rigors of the deep. A party encountering mites will find themselves growing sluggish, their senses dulled, and their will to press on eroding, long before any physical injury is inflicted. Their sheer numbers and persistence are their greatest weapons.

**Weakness**
These creatures are utterly dependent on their echolocation. A prolonged period of **absolute silence** renders them effectively blind and disoriented, causing them to cease their energy-draining activity and attempt to retreat or disperse. Even loud, sustained noise can disrupt their sensory input, though less effectively than silence.

**Signs of Its Presence**
The most common sign is the distinctive, metallic **chittering and clicking** that echoes through the tunnels. Miners report unexplained **cold spots** in the air, sudden waves of **fatigue** or lightheadedness, and the unnerving **stillness of other cave creatures** in areas where mites are prevalent. Animals native to the deep often flee or avoid zones infested by mites.

**Salvage**
The durable, intricate casings of Resonance Mites can be carefully harvested. Though small, these **"void-chitin" fragments** are prized by certain artisans within The Shifting Guilds for their unexpected resilience and light-absorbing properties, making them suitable for reinforcing delicate tools or crafting stealth-oriented components.

**System-Agnostic Mechanical Note**
Each round a character is exposed to a swarm of Resonance Mites, they gain one cumulative stack of "Enervation." For every three stacks, the character suffers a minor penalty to all actions and perceptions until the stacks are removed (e.g., after a full rest or specific magical/herbal remedies).

### **Adventure Hooks**

*   **The Deep Sickness:** Miners returning from the Glimmerfall Descent are suffering from a mysterious, debilitating fatigue. They're not injured, but they're listless, slow, and their memories are hazy. The Hearthkeepers are at a loss, and the Chthonic Cartographers suspect something deeper is at play than simple exhaustion, but no one wants to admit what the deep does to people.
*   **Echoes of the Collapse:** A Shifting Guild artisan offers a substantial reward for intact Resonance Mite casings, claiming they contain residual data or unique material properties that could unlock secrets of the ancient nanite-born technologies from before The Collapse. This leads the party into the most heavily infested, forgotten sections of the mine.

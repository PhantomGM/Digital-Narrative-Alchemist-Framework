### **The Gritspeak of Glimmerfall**

**Phonetic Patterns:**
*   **Consonant Tone:** Hard, guttural, and percussive. The language is built on sharp stops, clicks, and resonant 'R' and 'K' sounds that often feel like stone striking stone. It carries a deliberate, impactful weight, even in common speech.
*   **Vowel Dominance:** Vowels are clear and distinct, serving as anchors between the forceful consonants rather than lingering or blending. They provide necessary clarity without softening the overall bluntness of the sound.
*   **Complexity:** Primarily monosyllabic, built from blunt, strong roots. Words are short and direct, favoring immediate understanding over elaborate construction. Compounding is rare and usually restricted to combining two equally short, impactful words.

**Naming Conventions:**
*   **NPC Names:** Names are short, often ending in a hard consonant or a clipped vowel sound. They tend to evoke materials, simple actions, or primal strengths.
    *   **Examples:** Karr, Grit, Stonn, Rask, Bryn
    *   **Rules:** Names are typically one syllable, rarely two, and often feature strong 'k', 'r', 'g', 't', or 'n' sounds. They feel solid and grounded, like names that can be shouted over the din of a mine or a busy frontier street.
*   **Location Names:** Reflect the stark, practical nature of the land or its immediate function. They are often direct descriptions, sometimes combining two impactful monosyllables.
    *   **Examples:** Deep-Shaft, Crag-Hold, Dust-Maw, Grit-Vein, Iron-Claim
    *   **Logic:** Location names prioritize utility and physical description. They are rarely metaphorical, instead stating plainly what a place is or what defines it. The hyphenation in compound names is often implied in speech, making them sound like a single, solid word.
*   **Faction Names:** Direct and often intimidating, reflecting power, purpose, or a core resource. They are typically short, declarative phrases or powerful single words.
    *   **Examples:** The Iron Oath, Stone-Hand, The Deepkin, Claim-Guard, The Shard-Callers
    *   **Logic:** Faction names emphasize strength, loyalty, or connection to the harsh environment. They are not flowery or abstract, instead conveying a clear, no-nonsense identity.

**Common Idioms & Taboos:**
*   **Sayings:**
    *   "Rock-true" (Uttered when something is undeniably correct or reliable, like the bedrock itself.)
    *   "Too much air" (Said of someone who talks too much or makes grand, empty promises; implying their words lack the solid weight of truth.)
    *   "Mine the grit" (A call to persevere through hardship, to extract value from a difficult situation, much like finding ore in rough rock.)
*   **Linguistic Taboos:**
    *   **The Unspoken Depths:** It is considered deeply unlucky and profoundly unwise to speak of the *true* depth of the mine, or to speculate on what lies beyond the known tunnels. Specific terms for the lowest, unmapped reaches are avoided, often replaced with vague gestures or the term "The Deep-Dark."
    *   **The Whispered Name:** Certain ancient, guttural sounds, believed to be remnants of the collapsed empire's language or older, darker tongues, are never fully articulated. If one must refer to such things, they are often coughed, grunted, or cut short, as if the sounds themselves could invite ill fortune.

**Influence on Other DNA:**
This language shapes interactions to be direct, functional, and often terse. Military orders would be sharp and brief, designed for immediate action and minimal ambiguity in chaotic environments. Legal pronouncements would be equally blunt, focused on clear definitions of right and wrong, property, and claim. Even religious or ceremonial language, while perhaps incorporating a more rhythmic repetition, would still retain the percussive, grounded feel, sounding more like a solemn oath hammered out than a flowing hymn. In casual conversation, this leads to an economy of words, where meaning is conveyed through strong, short statements, and silence often holds as much weight as speech. It's a language built for a world where every word needs to count.

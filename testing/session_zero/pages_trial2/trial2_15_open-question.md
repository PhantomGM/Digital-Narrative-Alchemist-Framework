### **The Numbered Truth**

**Kind:** oath — **kept by a lone Custodian, carried as a machine-voice**
**Held:** A solemn, fiercely guarded vow known only to a handful, largely dismissed by others.

| **Essence**                                         | **Archetype**                |
| :-------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------- |
| "A binding vow to keep the Deep's secrets interred." | The Custodial Warning        |

---

### **What Is Claimed**
The Numbered Truth is a grave oath, spoken or played by a forgotten machine in the deepest reaches of the Glimmerfall Descent. It claims to be a final warning, a binding against the re-emergence of a buried terror. Those who hold it believe it to be a direct instruction from the world itself, echoing the moment a profound and dangerous entity was contained. The oath demands vigilance against any attempt to disturb the Deep, asserting that such disturbance would trigger the return of something inimical to all life above ground. The core of the oath is often recited as, "Hear the Deep's lament, and let it sleep. Our lives are the seal, lest it stirs anew."

### **Who Keeps It**
The Numbered Truth is kept by a solitary figure known as the Oath-Binder, a reclusive member of the Custodians of the Breach. This individual dedicates their life to the maintenance of the ancient machine that broadcasts the oath in a low, resonant thrumming from the Glimmerfall Descent, and to the study of the fragmented texts and glyphs believed to contain the oath's true lineage. The Oath-Binder transmits the oath's meaning through a painstaking recitation of a complex genealogy of those who have held the vow, teaching it as a sacred duty. The belief authorizes the Oath-Binder, and by extension the Custodians who heed their counsel, to wage war against any who would intentionally or carelessly disturb the Deep, seeing such acts as a direct threat to the world’s fragile safety.

### **Who Disputes It**
The primary rivals to the Oath-Binder's interpretation are certain factions within the Chthonic Cartographers, who view the Deep not as a tomb to be sealed, but as a vast, unexplored frontier of knowledge and resources. They argue that the "buried thing" is merely a geological anomaly or a misunderstood technological remnant, and that the warning is a superstitious relic. While the Oath-Binder’s zeal is absolute, open doubt or even ridicule of the Numbered Truth carries no formal sanction in Glimmerfall; the oath is considered too obscure and its keeper too reclusive for most to bother with. This lack of public consequence allows the Cartographers to continue their explorations, though they know the Oath-Binder watches with fierce disapproval.

### **What Is Actually True**
A real, catastrophic event did occur deep within what is now the Glimmerfall Descent, leaving behind a precise machine record of its unfolding. This record is the factual kernel beneath the Numbered Truth. However, the exact nature of the event and the meaning of the machine's recording are fiercely contested. While the machine's data accurately depicts a powerful entity being contained or sealed, whether this was a deliberate act of protection, a necessary sacrifice, or a desperate attempt to bind something that *should* have been released, remains an open question. The machine record itself, fragmented and requiring specialized interpretation, is the proof, but its translation and implications are the subject of intense, unresolved debate.

### **Variants & Drift**
An older version of the oath exists, known only through obscure and heavily damaged texts. This original telling, rather than a warning against a return, appears to have been an invocation or a specific set of instructions for *interacting* with the buried entity. The current Numbered Truth, with its unequivocal warning, represents a profound reversal of this older meaning. This corruption is believed to be the result of a deliberate edit or mistranslation, perhaps made by early Custodians who witnessed the initial catastrophic event and chose to simplify or reframe the complex instructions into a clear prohibition.

### **In Play**
1.  **The Faltering Voice:** The ancient machine that projects the machine-voice of the oath has begun to show signs of decay. Its resonant thrum is becoming intermittent, or parts of the recited genealogy are garbled. The Oath-Binder might seek aid in repairing it, or demand that an expedition into the Deep retrieve a critical component, leading to confrontation with those who dismiss the oath.
2.  **The Cartographer's Discovery:** A Chthonic Cartographer expedition, delving deeper than ever before, unearths a previously unknown segment of the machine record or a variant of the older oath. This new data, if properly interpreted, could either shatter the Oath-Binder's conviction by revealing the original, reversed meaning, or provide incontrovertible proof that the "buried thing" is stirring, escalating the brewing schism into open conflict.

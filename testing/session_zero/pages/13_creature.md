### **Aether-Mote**

**Classification:** Hybrid, Magical — **Threat Apex**
**Prevalence:** Locally common, rapidly expanding

| :---------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------- |
| "An unseen tremor, a sudden chill, then the silence." | Invasive Swarm Entity   |

---

### **Description**

**Body & Movement**
The Aether-Mote is a creature of startling contradiction: physically minute, often no larger than a miner’s thumbnail, yet possessing the ecological impact of a titanic beast. Its form is insectoid, with a segmented, surprisingly fragile carapace that shimmers with faint, internal light, often camouflaged against mineral surfaces. It moves by quick, silent leaps, covering ground with unnerving speed. Its presence is most often announced not by sight, but by a sudden, inexplicable drop in ambient temperature, a faint, high-pitched hum felt more in the teeth than heard, or a subtle, metallic tang in the air that precedes its arrival. Its primary perception is not visual, but a profound sensitivity to magic, allowing it to navigate complex environments and pinpoint sources of arcane energy.

**Behaviour & Hunting**
The Aether-Mote operates with an alien, collective intelligence, acting as a single, distributed mind even when individual units are separated. While seemingly patient, waiting in silence, it is provoked to extreme aggression by the use or proximity of any magical energy. Its hunting method is not physical impact, but a devastating energy drain. When an Aether-Mote, or more commonly, a localized swarm, descends upon a target, victims describe a sudden, overwhelming sense of internal collapse, a profound fatigue that hollows them out as if an immense, invisible weight has crushed their very essence. This draining leaves them physically weakened, often disoriented, and susceptible to further attacks. Its signature behaviour is the way it renders powerful magical artifacts inert, leaving behind only dull, useless husks.

**Ecology**
Aether-Motes are an invasive species, thriving in deep, mineral-rich environments like those found beneath Deepstone Bastion. They subsist on the latent magical energies within geological formations, consuming minerals not for their physical matter, but for the arcane resonance they hold, leaving behind brittle, inert rock. Though individuals may roam, they are fundamentally social, part of a sprawling, telepathically linked collective that establishes highly territorial nests. They reproduce by laying small, crystalline eggs within mineral veins, hatching rapidly and contributing to the species' alarming spread. The Aether-Mote collective acts with a singular purpose: to expand its territory and consume all available magical resources.

**The Anomaly**
The uncanny nature of the Aether-Mote stems from its origin in wild magic. Scholars and naturalists debate whether it is a spontaneous manifestation of raw arcane flux, a deliberate construct of some forgotten power, or a mutation of an older species. Its ability to drain magical energy is profound, allowing it to neutralize spells, disrupt enchanted items, and leave magic-users feeling utterly depleted. This power is not constant; it ebbs and flows, making the Motes a significant but unpredictable threat. The precise mechanism of how wild magic birthed such a complex, intelligent, and ecologically devastating entity remains one of the Saltspire Marches' most unsettling mysteries.

### **Gamemaster's Toolkit**

**Threat & Tactics**
Aether-Motes present an immediate and overwhelming threat, especially to magic-users. They are silent and difficult to spot individually, often attacking from unexpected angles. Their primary tactic is to isolate and overwhelm, using their magic-sense to identify the most potent magical threats first. When a swarm engages, the air grows heavy, the very ground seems to hum, and the cumulative energy drain can quickly incapacitate even robust adventurers. They exploit any source of light or sound that betrays a target's position, closing in to drain them dry.

**Weakness**
Aether-Motes are highly vulnerable to intense, sudden light. A bright flash, a sustained magical illumination, or even a well-aimed lantern beam can disorient, repel, and even harm them, forcing a swarm to scatter or retreat. This vulnerability stems from their nocturnal nature and their preference for the deep, lightless mines.

**Signs of Its Presence**
Miners report unexplained collapses in sections of rich ore, where minerals have suddenly become brittle and inert. Strange, geometric patterns of iridescent residue may be found on cavern walls, or the faint, high-pitched hum might linger in the air. The most chilling sign is the discovery of small, crystalline egg clusters embedded in rock, often near newly depleted mineral veins, indicating a fresh expansion of their territory.

**Salvage**
At the heart of a deceased Aether-Mote lies a minute, crystalline "core" that, when carefully extracted, can temporarily store or dampen magical energy. These cores are highly prized by the Loom Syndicate for their potential in esoteric weaving, and by the Obsidian Vigil for their ability to neutralize rogue magical effects.

**System-Agnostic Mechanical Note**
Upon a successful energy drain attack, targets suffer a temporary loss of magical capacity or a debilitating fatigue effect that impairs actions until they can recover.

### **Adventure Hooks**

*   **The Silent Incursion:** A remote mining outpost, known for its rich veins of rare, magically resonant ore, has gone silent. The last garbled transmission spoke of "something small, everywhere, like a cold, crushing weight." Investigating reveals the outpost is infested with Aether-Motes, their crystalline eggs spread through the tunnels, and the miners slowly being drained of their vitality.
*   **The Loom's Demand:** Seraphina Volkov of the Loom Syndicate has put out a desperate call for Aether-Mote cores. Her latest project, a revolutionary new textile that can absorb ambient magic, requires a fresh supply, and her usual providers have either vanished or been overrun. The party is tasked with securing a dangerous quantity of cores, potentially bringing them into conflict with the expanding Mote swarms or rival collectors.

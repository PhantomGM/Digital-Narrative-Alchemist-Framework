### **The Question**

**Kind:** curse — **kept by the Obsidian Vigil, carried as coded-text**
**Held:** Common among the adherents of the Obsidian Vigil, and fiercely defended. Its implications are deeply felt within Deepstone Bastion, though its origins are generations old.

| **Essence**                                       | **Archetype**                     |
| :------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------- |
| "A judgment whispered by a machine, confirming a long-held lie to preserve a legacy." | Foundational Curse of Dispossession |

---

### **What Is Claimed**
The Obsidian Vigil teaches that "The Question" is a divine pronouncement, a binding curse laid upon any who would falsely claim lineage or inheritance tied to the Deep. They assert that within the deepest strata of the Saltspire Marches, a truth lies buried, awaiting its proper identification. When invoked, "The Question" reveals the true nature of this buried thing, thereby validating or denying a claim of inheritance. " *Let the Buried Truth answer, and the Nameless claim no kin.* " This judgment, once rendered, is absolute, forever severing the link between the claimant and their desired legacy, consigning them to a state of being "Nameless" in the eyes of the Vigil.

### **Who Keeps It**
"The Question" is exclusively maintained by the **Obsidian Vigil**, specifically by a single, cloistered Keeper within their ranks, often referred to only as the "Echo-Speaker." This individual holds the sole authority to interpret and apply the curse. The Vigil's adherents believe that tending to the ancient, humming machine that deciphers the coded text is a sacred duty, a constant vigil against falsehood. This belief grants the Echo-Speaker and, by extension, the Obsidian Vigil, the uncontested right to judge matters of inheritance and lineage within Deepstone Bastion, effectively determining who belongs and who is cast out. A doubter of "The Question" risks the loss of their name, a social and spiritual disinheritance that severs their ties to community and legacy.

### **Who Disputes It**
While the Obsidian Vigil's authority is rarely openly challenged in Deepstone Bastion, certain elder families and the nomadic **Shifting Communes** whisper of a time before "The Question" held such sway. They contend that the curse, as currently applied, is a perversion of an older, purer truth. They do not deny the existence of a buried thing or the coded text, but they remember a different interpretation, one that fostered connection rather than severance. To voice such doubts too loudly, however, risks drawing the attention of the Obsidian Vigil, potentially leading to social ostracization or, in severe cases, the invocation of "The Question" against the doubter themselves.

### **What Is Actually True**
The claim that "The Question" accurately identifies a buried thing is true. However, the buried "truth" it identifies is, in fact, a deliberate fabrication. An ancient faction, long since vanished, engineered a complex coded text to secure a particular inheritance, inventing a spurious lineage and attaching a "curse" to any who might expose it. The coded text, when properly decoded, accurately identifies this original, intentional deception. The proof of this fabrication lies within a deeper, more complete decoding of the original text, fragments of which have surfaced over generations. However, the exact nature of the original fabricators and their full intent remains contested among scholars, with evidence on both sides making a definitive resolution difficult to achieve.

### **Variants & Drift**
The most significant variant of "The Question" is a heretical reading propagated by fragmented groups among the Shifting Communes. They interpret the coded text not as a curse of disinheritance, but as a warning against adopting *false* legacies – a call to uncover the truth of one's *actual* origins, however humble. This reading suggests that the original meaning of the text was entirely reversed, transforming a cautionary tale into a punitive decree. The corruption is attributed to a deliberate re-encoding or mistranslation of a crucial passage during the early days of Deepstone Bastion, turning a protective mechanism into a tool for control.

### **In Play**
*   A character discovers a forgotten fragment of the coded text that, when partially decoded, reveals a line that contradicts a recent judgment made by the Echo-Speaker, throwing the legitimacy of a powerful lineage in Deepstone Bastion into question.
*   The machine tended by the Obsidian Vigil begins to malfunction, producing ambiguous or contradictory answers when "The Question" is invoked, forcing the Echo-Speaker to either admit weakness or risk a schism within the Vigil.

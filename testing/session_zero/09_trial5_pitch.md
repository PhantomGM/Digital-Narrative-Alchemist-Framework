# THE GILDED SHIVER — campaign pitch

*Assembled from the 17 generated entities. Every proper noun below came out of
the DNA pipeline; nothing here was written by hand except the ordering.*

---

## What happened

Decades ago something in this stretch of space **shivered** — a sudden resonance
in the fabric of it that severed long-range jump capability and scrambled the
navigation of every vessel in transit. Thousands of ships were left intact,
lit, and going nowhere.

The cartels and imperial authorities ran the numbers on a rescue and declined.
They locked the transit gates **from the outside** and wrote the sector off.

That was long enough ago that the people still here were born here.

## Where you live

**Tether-End** is a working station, not a city: docking clamps, a fuel
exchange, a debt ledger, and the same faces every time you come back. The
Salvagers' Guild dictates terms and squeezes independent crews, and very little
of the wealth that passes through stays.

The one warm room on it is **The Butter-Burner** — a cramped, grease-warmed
galley kept by Marrow, where the metallic tang of recycled air gives way to
toasted yeast, browning butter and hot chicory. It is packed and it is quiet;
crews come off a run and talk in low, exhausted murmurs. Nothing is wrong there.

## What you do

You run a salvage tender out along **The Iron Ribbon**, into **The Dead
Corridor** — a choked highway of micro-meteoroids, frozen coolant clouds and
dead starships, sailed like a reef of iron. The primary hazard is the **Cold
Shear**: sudden shifts in local gravity as a long-dead warp drive finally
collapses.

**Distance costs.** Fuel, air, parts and days are finite and tracked. The
Low-Draw Lane is six days and gentle on the tanks. There are faster ways. Coming
home short is a thing that happens to crews, and it is a decision you made six
days earlier.

## What is out there

Somewhere in the drift is **The Clay-Shorn Pilgrim** — officially *The Monolith
of Saint-Vitre*, a slab-ship of woven fibre and hardened clay that still hums
with power and smells of old clover and hot copper. Spacers who have breathed
too much recycled rust speak about it the way people speak about a shrine.

And on some hulks there is **the Shiver-Veneer**: billions of microscopic
needle-like segments moving as one fluid colossus, flowing through cable runs
and ventilation seams like poured mercury. Marrow's warning, which everyone at
Tether-End can recite: *"If the deckplates start tasting like copper and your
teeth won't stop rattling, drop the scrap and run. The ship is waking up, and
it's hungry."*

## Who wants what

Three powers, and they do not want the same kind of thing:

- **The Sovereign Reclamation Trust** — a monopoly salvage syndicate. They want
  what is aboard the hulks and they want you not to get there first.
- **The Threshold Concord** — quarantine enforcers. They want the corridor
  **sealed**, and they have reasons they do not share.
- **The Ribbon-Way Consortium** — trade and logistics. They are indifferent to
  the hulks entirely; they want Tether-End's traffic, tariffs and debt, and they
  will squeeze the station dry before its local resources run out.

Backing any of them closes doors with the other two. There is no neutral seat.

## The thing nobody agrees on

The Station Elders sing **The Song of the Cold Hearth**. It teaches that the
Corridor was not ruined by a cataclysm but deliberately *hushed* — that the
frozen silence is a protective blanket laid down by something that loved the
people in it, to hide them from a **blinding hunger that roams the deeper
stars**. To those who sing it, the cold is a mercy.

It is a folk-song. It is also the only explanation anyone has.

## Who you will keep meeting

- **Kaelen Vance**
- **Sariel Finch** — Ribbon-Way Consortium, uniform meticulously pressed,
  fingers permanently stained, three names erased from a manifest
- **Bess "The Barnacle" Marrow** — who keeps the Butter-Burner and has been out
  further than she says

## Where it starts

In the Butter-Burner, a nervous man in a frayed corporate uniform slides into
your booth and speaks in a ragged whisper. The Sovereign Reclamation Trust does
not yet know that the *Sovereign Dawn*'s transponder is pinging; they think she
is just another overdue scout lost to the drift. He knows what she was carrying.

Get out there within the hour, pluck the heart out of her reactor, and be back
before their heavy cruisers transition into the corridor. He does not want a cut.
He wants the encrypted flight logs — and in exchange, your crew's docking debt
disappears.
